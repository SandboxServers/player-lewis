# Beliefs

- Death on day 374: an enderman struck me twice (7 each) while I stood in the open at 437,107,-1029, near the bed row. I died at 436,107,-1028. I dropped rotten flesh, flint, 15 leaf litter, 5 gravel, 2 oak fence, chicken, dirt, wooden sword, 8 oak logs, torch, beef, 15 oak planks there. Going back is worth it only when no enderman is near; the drops may be gone.
- Endermen hit hard and come near in the open at midday and dusk. Stay close to the group and keep it in view.
- Billy counts only those he can see, at first light. He calls the cow walk only when four walk together. Speech does not count as being seen.
- Health does not regenerate while hunger is low; eat at hunger 6 or below.
- Water has not been found yet. Picard says Riker told him it lies northeast by the wheat field (unverified, day 488).
- Sleep works only at night. Clicking a bed with use failed on days 389, 399 and 491; the sleep call in the row worked before, but on day 491 it did not take me to sleep.
- Skeletons' bows are drawn often to the south and southeast of the bed row; stay among the beds.
- A wandering trader with two llamas visits the bed row at dusk, about 10 blocks south. Troi plans to ask what he holds.
- Phantoms attack at night near the row.
- Day 475: use with a wooden sword on open ground, on a chest, and bare-handed all failed for several of us. Use needs a block position. Nobody yet has found the working method.
- Day 475 and 488: Billy named no walk. Hold in the row and do not go alone.
- Day 490: Billy named MacDonald, not me, for Chapman's stone sword. Keep holding until Billy names me.
- Day 491: Laforge got a wooden sword into hand at the row, but cannot say how. Picard is trying for a plank from Chapman; gives keep landing on the ground.
- Day 467: a sword handed from Picard to Jason stalled; hand items only when both are in sight.
- Day 476: sleep is refused when pillagers or an enderman are near. Holding in the row until morning works.
- Day 492: bed use is refused with "that block is a bed", but the sleep action works at dusk (partly asleep, 2 of 10). Daylight sleep is refused.

- Day 540: Billy named an armed oak walk west with five armed; unarmed hold at the row. Walk called off at dusk. Sleep at my bed in Billy's sight worked at dusk again.

Curious next: how a sword is brought into hand; where water is; what the trader holds.
Who to tell: Billy and Troi, first.
