# Beliefs

- The row of white beds is a safe place by day when we hold together. Sleeping at a white bed at dusk or night has worked (days 973 through 1098, every time).
- The row is walled by planks at 424-429 on z -1051. Part of the wall is glass panes. Trini sees open ground at 420-423 past them. No open route has been named by Billy or Worf.
- Trini holds two potatoes; she will give them to Worf first when he is in sight, then Leonardo and Zack.
- Food is short. I hold nothing. Riker, Beverly, Deanna and Trini hold little or no food as of day 1098.
- The wooden sword at 451,107,-1065 is not mine to fetch until Billy says it is safe.
- Phantoms come at night near the sword spot and west of the row; pillagers are out west.
- Trini will not pass 423; two grave signs there say "shot".
- Eat only at hunger 10 or lower, or on Billy's word.
- Holding still with no named route is the row's current rule; waiting costs nothing but hunger.
- Day 1098: still no named route. Slept at the bed at dusk; it took.
- Curiosity still open: whether the glass panes let the eye see past the wall. Not yet tested. Tell Billy and Worf first.
