# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## What worked
- Sleep works only at night, and only in a bed with no monster in view and no one else in it. It worked on the nights of day 255 and day 267. Sleep at dusk before dark is refused ("not night").
- Going direct, one short goto at a time, was more reliable than long travel intents, which drifted or failed after one step.
- Sleep with the sleep action. When the bed use was refused, sleep from beside the bed sometimes worked (day 297 at -270,67,336 and at the yellow bed on day 300).
- Using the use action on ripe wheat was refused; the mine action was tried in its place. The result was not clearly recorded, so treat it as unproven.
- A wooden pickaxe can be crafted from oak logs with a table: planks, table, then pickaxe (around day 293). Crafting a wooden hoe also worked (day 268).
- Unstick got me out of a pit twice (day 301 and day 302).
- Wheat seeds need dry tilled farmland (wooden hoe) and take time to ripen.
- Killing a sheep near (-240,68,260) gave mutton; a chicken west of the beds gave one raw chicken.
- Eating works at hunger 13 (beef, day 276) and at 15 to 17 (porkchop, day 286).
- A give works only when the person is in reach and in sight. Keep the item and wait until they are close.
- The rule that held up: give to whoever is in reach and lowest in health.

## What did not work, and why
- ~~Sleeping on a free white bed works.~~ Sleep is refused when a creeper, phantom or golem is in view, or when the bed is crowded ("the way was blocked"). On days 290 to 302 it failed more often than it worked.
- ~~Iron golems stand 12 to 20 blocks south and have not attacked me.~~ Golems closed in from the south and west, and I was hit near them on day 280. Keep clear of them.
- ~~Food is scarce; meat goes to Trini first, then Worf and Data.~~ The fixed order never held. Give by reach and by lowest health instead.
- ~~A "rest" intent can finish at once.~~ Travel and gather intents also failed after one step ("changed nothing"). Use direct actions.
- ~~Using the bed click (use) is the way to sleep.~~ Refused more often than not; the sleep verb did better.
- ~~The chicken only eats at hunger 6 or below.~~ I ate at 13 and 15.
- ~~Oak sapling and short grass give food or seeds.~~ Gathering short grass gave nothing on days 255 and 256. Saplings and dirt are not food.
- Pig and cow hunts east and south (days 290 to 302) found no animals. Do not count on a hunt.
- Skeletons on the north-west grass near (-290,270) killed me on day 266. On days 300 and 301 their bows drew the group back from the west. Do not walk into their line of fire without cover.
- Attacking a zombie or a spider with an oak log, low on health, left me at health 5 (day 290). A fight at dusk with a weak weapon nearly killed me.
- Fighting while standing in water left me at health 9. Get out of water before fighting.
- Dying at night with an empty pack means respawning with nothing; the dropped tools are lost unless I can reach them first.
