# Beliefs

## What worked
- Sleeping at a white bed at dusk or night has worked every time (days 973 through 1153). A bed is the safest place I know.
- Holding in the group's sight by day, with an armed person in reach, kept me alive from day 1177 on, at the tables and the cow spot.
- Asking by name for planks, sticks or a sword, in reach, got me things: MacGyver gave 4 planks and sticks; Archimede gave a stone sword after I respawned.
- Crafting a wooden sword at a table from planks and sticks works.

## What did not work, and why
- Walking alone, unarmed, out of the group at night: killed me twice (pillagers south of the row) and nearly again by skeletons at -938,84,-2927 (day 1197, health under 1). Never walk out at night, and never alone.
- Fighting phantoms and skeletons at dusk or night in the open: my health went from 20 to 4 more than once at the tables.
- Holding still with no named route: hunger went from 19 to 20 with nothing gained, and I have had no bed. Holding costs hunger.
- Sending a worker errand for status or wait: refused every time, its world tools resolve to nothing. Do not send it again.
- Walking to my dropped items at -955,78,-3013: the world refused the walk. Do not repeat it without an armed escort in sight and a reason.
- Waiting in the row for a named route: no route came, and the row was left behind.
