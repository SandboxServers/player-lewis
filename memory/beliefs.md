# Beliefs

- The row of white beds is a safe place by day when we hold together. Sleeping at a white bed at dusk or night has worked (days 973 through 1083, every time).
- The row is walled by planks at 424-429 on z -1051. Part of the wall is glass panes (seen from 444,107,-1051 facing west). Trini sees open ground at 420-423 past them. No open route has been named by Billy or Worf.
- Trini holds two potatoes toward Leonardo, Worf and Zack; she hands them out only when they are in her sight.
- Food is short. I hold nothing. Riker, Beverly, Deanna and Trini also hold little or no food as of day 1083.
- The wooden sword at 451,107,-1065 is not mine to fetch until Billy says it is safe.
- Phantoms come at night near the sword spot and west of the row; pillagers are out west.
- Trini will not pass 423; two grave signs there say "shot".
- Eat only at hunger 10 or lower, or on Billy's word.
- Holding still with no named route is the row's current rule; waiting costs nothing but hunger.
- Day 1083: still no named route. Morning light, then night. Slept at the bed at night; it took.
- Curiosity still open: whether the row's glass panes let the eye see past the wall. Not yet tested. Tell Billy and Worf first.
