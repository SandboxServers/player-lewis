# Beliefs

- The row of white beds is a safe place by day when we hold together. Sleeping at a white bed at dusk or night has worked (days 973, 997, 998, 1003, 1004).
- The row is walled by planks. No open cell has been named yet by Billy or Worf (still true day 1004).
- Food is short. Nobody in the row holds food I know of. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack.
- The wooden sword at 451,107,-1065 is not mine to fetch until Billy says it is safe.
- Phantoms come at night near the sword spot and west of the row; pillagers are out west.
- Eat only at hunger 10 or lower, or on Billy's word.
- Holding still with no named route is the row's current rule; waiting costs nothing but hunger.
- Curiosity still open: whether the row's walls can be seen past, and what lies beyond the planks. Tell Billy and Worf first.
