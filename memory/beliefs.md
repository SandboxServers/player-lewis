# Beliefs

- The row of white beds is a safe place by day when we hold together. Sleeping at a white bed at dusk has worked every time.
- The row is walled by planks. No open cell has been named yet.
- Food is short. Nobody in the row holds food; Trini held potatoes for Leonardo.
- The wooden sword at 451,107,-1065 is not mine to fetch until Billy says it is safe.
- Phantoms come at night near the sword spot and west of the row; pillagers are out west.
- Eat only at hunger 10 or lower, or on Billy's word.
