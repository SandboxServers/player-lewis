# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## What worked
- Sleep at a free white bed can work, but only when no monster is close and no one else is in the bed. Sleep worked on the night of day 255 and on the night of day 267, and failed many other times.
- Wheat seeds need tilled farmland (wooden hoe) and take time to ripen. Tilling fails if a block is above the spot; the seed then cannot be placed where unripe wheat stands ("target occupied").
- A give works only when the person is in reach and in sight. Keep the item and wait until they are close.
- Sleep directly with the sleep action; the "use" click on a bed was refused more often than not.
- Crafting a wooden hoe needs planks, sticks and a table; it worked near (-285,66,321) on day 268.
- Killing a sheep near (-240,68,260) gave mutton; a chicken west of the beds gave one raw chicken.
- Eating works at hunger 13 (beef, day 276) and at 15 to 17 (porkchop, day 286). ~~The chicken only eats at hunger 6 or below.~~ (False: I ate at 13 and 15.)

## What did not work, and why
- ~~Sleeping on a free white bed works.~~ Sleep is refused when a creeper, phantom or golem is in view, or when the bed is crowded ("the way was blocked"). Often I drifted far from the bed on the way.
- ~~Iron golems stand 12 to 20 blocks south and have not attacked me.~~ False in part: golems closed in from the south and west, and I was hit near the golems on day 280. Keep clear of them.
- ~~Food is scarce; meat goes to Trini first, then Worf and Data.~~ The fixed order did not hold. The rule that worked was to give to whoever is in reach and lowest in health.
- ~~A "rest" intent can finish at once. Sleep directly instead.~~ Travel and gather intents also failed after one step ("changed nothing", "travel failed after one step"). A direct goto was more reliable.
- Gather of short grass for seeds gave nothing on day 255 and day 256.
- Skeletons on the north-west grass near (-290,270) killed me on day 266. Do not walk into their line of fire without cover.
- Attacking a zombie while standing in water left me at health 9. Get out of water before fighting.
