# Day 901

Woke at bed 443,107,-1051, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Held at the bed all day, as Billy had named no walk. Heard the row check in: Beverly holds 10 potatoes owed to Zack and Worf; Riker at health 11 and hunger 17 with no food; Deanna at health 20; William holding. Nothing was asked of me. Said good morning and held. Slept in my bed at night.
