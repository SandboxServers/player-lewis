# Day 1107

Held in the row at my white bed 444,107,-1051 all day. Hunger stayed at 17, health steady at 13, pack empty. Nobody named a daylight route, so I did not walk. Trini carried two potatoes toward 423 for Worf and Zack; Leonardo was reported at 1 health and I had no food to give. Beverly, Deanna and Riker held their beds and listened for Billy. I slept beside my bed at dusk with the sleep verb.
