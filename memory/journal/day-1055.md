# Day 1055

Woke at my white bed 444,107,-1051 in the row, health 13, hunger 17, pack empty. Nothing new was named: no daylight route from Billy or Worf. I viewed the row's west wall: planks, with glass panes two blocks left and down-left, so part of the wall is glass. Leonardo was reported at health 1; Trini holds the two potatoes for him first, then Worf and Zack. I told the row I hold nothing and stay put. Slept at my bed at night without trouble.
