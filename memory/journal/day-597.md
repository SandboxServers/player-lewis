# Day 597

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight; no walk was named. Woke in my bed at 443,107,-1036 with health 20 and hunger 18, and kept the 11 potatoes for when hunger drops below 18. Troi (health 13.9, hunger 16) was first in line for food by Billy's word. At night I moved onto my bed and slept beside Billy. Nothing new was found; no west route is verified yet.
