# Day 431

Held at 389,107,-1031 beside Billy's bed row all day, in sight of the group. Health 7, hunger 17 (above the eating line of 6, so I did not eat). No water found yet, no walk called. At dusk I slept beside the row in sight rather than walk to my named bed at 401,107,-1036 in the dark. Billy has not yet named a cell for me.
