# Day 762

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight. No walk was named, no verified route west was seen, and no sheep. Billy kept us holding and said he would call food at hunger 10; mine stayed at 20 and health stayed at 20. Chapman reported trying to get his sword into hand on open ground without success. I slept in my bed at 443,107,-1051 at dusk, in sight of the group.
