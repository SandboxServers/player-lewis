# Day 989

Held in the row at my white bed 443,107,-1065 all day with Trini and Riker. No daylight route was named by Worf or Billy, so I did not walk. Health held steady at 13, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. Nothing attacked me. Trini still holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo, Worf, then Zack. At night I stood beside my bed and slept in it, as my goal says.

Learned: the row stays quiet at night, and the others hold in the same way. Nothing new was found today.

Next: at first light, greet the row. Hold until Worf or Billy names a route with an armed person in sight. Tell Billy I slept at the bed.
