# Journal
- 02:15 Woke in my bed at 387,107,-1065 in the row, night of day 470. Health 7, hunger 17. Slept again; the night passed and the sleep worked. Nothing new named by Billy. Still unarmed, no water found. Next: stay in the row, eat at hunger 6, ask Billy about the sword and water.
- Night, day 470. In my bed in the row, health 7, hunger 17. Pillagers near the row; Tommy, Picard, Wesley and Chapman all holding at their beds, no walk. I hold too and go to sleep here.
