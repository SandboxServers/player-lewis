# Day 1077

Held in the row all day beside the white bed at 444,107,-1051. No route named by Billy or Worf, so no walk. Health steady at 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Nobody has food to spare yet; Trini holds two potatoes for Worf and Zack. Riker, Beverly, Troi and Trini all held by their beds and spoke in the row. Slept at the white bed at night; sleep worked.
