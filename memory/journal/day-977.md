# Day 977

Held in the row all day at my white bed 443,107,-1065. Health steady at 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. No daylight route was named, so I did not walk. Riker, Trini and William kept their beds too. Trini still holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack. I slept beside my bed at night. Nothing new was found; the row is still walled by planks.
