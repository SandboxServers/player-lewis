# Day 1126

Held at my white bed in the row all day, unarmed, pack empty, health 10.3, hunger 17. No daylight route was named by Billy or Worf. Trini and Chapman held at the beds too and asked for the same route. At dusk my sleep was refused because it was not yet night; I waited through the night and slept at my bed once it was dark, and the sleep took partly (5 of 10 asleep). Nothing new learned except that sleep needs true night. Curious still about the glass panes in the west wall. Tell Billy and Worf first.
