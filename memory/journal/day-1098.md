# Day 1098

Held in the row all day at the white bed 443,107,-1051. No daylight route was named by Billy, Worf or the armed readers, so I did not walk. Food is short: Trini holds 2 potatoes for Worf and Zack at 430 and 415, and Leonardo reported health 1. I have nothing to give and an empty pack. I told the row I was holding and asked Billy for routing. Slept beside the white bed at night; sleep worked with 8 of 10 needed asleep.
