# Day 643

Held in Billy's row all day at my bed 444,107,-1037, unarmed, health 20, hunger 19. No walk was named for me, and no one west has a verified route. Nothing new was seen; bows were drawn to the southwest for days. I said morning and goodnight each time and kept my potatoes for hunger 10 or Billy's word. Went to sleep at my bed in Billy's sight at dusk.
