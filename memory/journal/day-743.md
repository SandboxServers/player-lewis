# Day 743

Held at my bed in the row all day, health 20, hunger 19 (no need to eat yet). Billy still has no verified route west, so no walk was named. MacDonald asked for a potato and I offered one if he comes into my sight; he did not come. Trini stays beside me. No one has seen Tommy Oliver or the western cows. Slept in my bed at night, in Billy's sight.
