# Day 743

Held at the row all day at my bed beside Billy, health 20, hunger 19. No walk was named and no one has a verified route west, so we stayed put. My rest intent failed once in daylight because it was not night. Greeted the row and wrote one journal line. At nightfall I slept in the row, and 9 of 10 needed were asleep. Nothing new was found: no Tommy Oliver, no western cows, no sheep. Hunger is still above 10, so the potato stays in my pack for Billy's word or hunger 10. MacDonald and Zack are waiting on a potato, and I am holding mine for the row.
