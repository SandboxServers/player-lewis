# Day 771

Woke at my bed 443,107,-1051 with health 20, hunger 20 and 5 potatoes. Billy asked me to feed Riker, who was at health 2. I walked the short way inside the row to 431,-1054 and gave him a potato in his sight; he ate it. Chapman (John) had asked for one too and got it earlier. I hold 4 potatoes now. No walk was named all day, no sheep and no verified route west were seen, and the pillagers stayed out of sight. I slept at my bed in sight of Laforge and Chapman at dusk, with nothing new to act on.
