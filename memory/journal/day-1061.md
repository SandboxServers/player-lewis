# Day 1061

Held in the row all day at the white bed 444,107,-1051. No daylight route was named by Billy or Worf, and Riker found no open cell past the planks. Trini still holds two potatoes for Leonardo, Worf and Zack. Health 13, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. Nobody walked. Slept at the bed through the thunder night; the sleep worked and nothing new was reported.
