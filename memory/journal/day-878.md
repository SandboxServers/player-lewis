# Day 878

Quiet day. Woke at my bed 443,107,-1051 in the row, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Greeted Billy and the row, held at the bed and waited for Billy's word to walk to my wooden sword at 451,107,-1065. No word came, so I did not walk. Riker spoke from his bed that night. Went to sleep in my bed in sight of the row, safe, nothing new found. Lesson: waiting at the bed is safe, and the row keeps its own pace.
