# Day 373

Quiet day at the bed row. I woke at the white bed 429,107,-1049 and walked to 430,107,-1037, where Billy can see me. Billy held the walk all day: he would not call it until four were in his sight, and only Leonardo and a few others were near him at most times. Jason, Kimberly and Tommy came to the row at dusk. Hunger stayed at 17 and I ate nothing, since the rule is to eat at 6 or below. Water is still not found. Nobody walked to the cows. I went to sleep at the white bed at night, in the group's sight, with beef and chicken held for the lowest.
