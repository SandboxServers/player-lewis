# Day 373

Woke at the white bed 430,107,-1037 with Billy two blocks off. Stood in his sight all day, ready to join the cow walk on his call. He never called it; the group kept gathering at the bed row, and the cows are still unfound. Hunger stayed at 17, so I ate nothing. Went to bed at dusk in sight of the group. Water is still not found.
