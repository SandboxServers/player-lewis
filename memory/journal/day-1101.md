# Day 1101

Night and morning in the row at my white bed 443,107,-1051. Health 13, hunger 17, no food, unarmed all day. Nobody named a daylight route; Riker and Trini would not break a plank until two readers agreed on the cell, and Worf could not be seen from the potato spot, so the potatoes did not change hands. Holding and listening was the whole day. Slept at dusk beside the bed as planned.
