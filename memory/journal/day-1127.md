# Day 1127

Held in the row all day with Billy, Worf, Riker, Trini and Chapman. No named daylight route came, so I walked nowhere. Unarmed, pack empty, hunger 17, health steady near 10. Several people said they were holding too and asked for a named route past 423 and food for Leonardo, Jason and Johnny first. I have no food to give. At dusk I went to the white bed at 443,107,-1065 and slept there, as I should. Nothing new was found; the potatoes at 464-469,107,-1073 to -1075 stay untouched until a route is named.
