# Day 967

Woke at my white bed 430,107,-1051, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Still walled by planks in the row; no daylight route or open cell named by Worf or Billy. Held at the bed all day, in sight of the row. Food is short: Worf at hunger 9, Leonardo at health 1, Trini holding 2 potatoes for him, Beverly hungry. I have no food to give. Dusk came, I slept at my bed beside the row with no danger near.
