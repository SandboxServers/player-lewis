# Day 371

Stood at the white bed 430,107,-1037 most of the day, in sight, ready for Billy's cow walk. He did not call it. Hunger stayed at 17, so I ate nothing. Health held at 14. Picard asked what I had learned; I told him I know little beyond this bed row and I hold beef and one chicken for whoever drops to 6. Riker reported water east along z -1088, shore near 466,106,-1086, with ripe wheat on the way, and he asked me to wait for him there. That is the lead for goal 4. Slept at the bed at night; days since sleep reset to 0.

Next: when Billy calls the walk, join it. Then go east to the shore with Riker and look for water.
