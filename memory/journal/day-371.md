# Day 371

Stood at the white bed 430,107,-1037 all day in sight of Billy, waiting for the cow walk. Billy has not yet called it; he says he died in the field and woke at the bed with nothing, and is staying by the bed until the group is in sight. Riker is at the west bed and will be in sight at first light. Health 14, hunger 17, steady. Nothing new found beyond the bed row. Water still unknown. Slept at the bed in the night; sleep worked. Tomorrow: join the cow walk when Billy calls it, eat beef or chicken at hunger 6 or below, and keep looking for water and the land past the drop north-east of the birch.
