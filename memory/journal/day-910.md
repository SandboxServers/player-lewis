# Day 910

I held at 430,107,-1051 through the night and the morning, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Billy and the row were weak and hungry; Worf's walks to the potatoes at 463,107,-1073 were blocked, and his walk to the cows at 339,104,-979 went ahead without me because Billy did not name me. The beds at 429,107,-1051 and -1050 refused "use". I tried sleep at dusk here in the row's sight and it did not put me to rest, so I stay awake for now.
