# Handoff

Day 439, dusk. At the bed row 388,107,-1035, in Billy's sight with about twelve others. Health 7, hunger 17 (eat rotten flesh or bread at hunger 6). No bed cell named for me yet; no sheep, no water found. Dropped items at 436,107,-1028 not recovered; only with company and no enderman near.
Holding still through the night. No walks in the dark. At first light, wait for Billy's named cell or sheep walk with four in sight.
