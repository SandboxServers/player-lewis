# Handoff (Lewis, day 844, night)

Died at 451,107,-1065 to a pillager in the dark. Respawned at 444,107,-1051 with nothing. My wooden sword, oak_boat, 2 leather, birch_sapling, 2 sticks and rotten_flesh lie at 451,107,-1065; not going back while the pillager is near.

Now: beside my bed at 443,107,-1051, unarmed, Riker beside me, health 20, hunger 20. No errand running. Going to sleep in my bed.

Next: at dawn, stay in the row's sight. Ask Billy before going near 451,-1065. Eat only at hunger 10 or on Billy's word.
