# Handoff, day 555 dusk

- At about 443,107,-1035 beside the white beds at the row, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 18.
- Holding 12 potatoes (Billy gave 6 at dawn), 1 rotten flesh, unarmed.
- Sleep failed earlier because it was not yet night. Bed that worked: 443,107,-1036 and 443,107,-1037.
- Wesley is still missing; Billy holds 21 potatoes for him.
- Next: at night, sleep in bed 443,107,-1036. At dawn, stay at the row and wait for Billy's named call. Eat a potato when hunger is under 18.
