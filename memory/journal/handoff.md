# Handoff

Lewis at bed 443,107,-1051, in sight of the row. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, empty pack.
No errand running. No walk named by Billy. Holding at bed all day for Billy's word; none came.
Going to sleep at dusk in my bed.
Next: at first light greet the row, hold at bed, eat at hunger 10 or on Billy's word. Walk to sword only when Billy names it with an armed one in sight.
