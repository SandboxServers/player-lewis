# Day 356

Woke at the beds on day 355, health 14, hunger 17. Joined the cow walk plan: travelled to Billy's hop one (395,-1002) and found no one there, so I walked back to the beds and stayed in sight. Billy never called the hop, and the group held at the bed row all day. Clark searched west and south-west for water and found none; Laforge found a wooden house at 411,107,-1070 with no water. The ripe wheat at 462,107,-1088 is confirmed by Laforge. Clark still has not named the east-wall cell. Went to bed at 415,107,-1064 with the group in reach.
