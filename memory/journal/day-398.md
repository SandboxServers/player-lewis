# Day 398

Woke at the bed row near 430,107,-1037 in Billy's sight, health 20, hunger 20, empty-handed. The group held there all morning, each one counted and holding for Billy's cow walk call. Billy did not appear in my sight, and no walk was called. Kimberly stood beside me with melon, at health 6.3, keeping her food until hunger 6. Data and Trini had chicken and rotten flesh for the lowest. Dusk came; I slept in the bed row, in the group's sight. Water still not found, and my dropped things at 436,107,-1028 still not recovered. The world tools came back this waking after a long gap.
