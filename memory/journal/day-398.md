# Day 398

Quiet night at the bed row, 430,107,-1037. Health and hunger full, empty-handed. Kimberly stood in reach with a melon slice; Leonardo called out from his own bed at 401,107,-1023 and asked to be counted at first light. I did not go out, did not dig, and slept in the bed. Nothing new was found today. The dropped things at 436,107,-1028 are still unrecovered, and water is still not found.
