# Day 992

Held in the row all day at my white bed 443,107,-1065. No route was named by Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. Trini and Riker also held at their beds; Riker plans to fetch the potatoes only when Worf names a walk. Nothing hurt me. Health 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. At dusk I said goodnight to the row and slept at my bed, as I have every night so far.
