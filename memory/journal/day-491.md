# Day 491

Stayed in the row at my bed 387,107,-1065, in Billy's sight, all day. Health 7, hunger 17 the whole time; nothing changed. No walk named, no sword given to me. Picard and Chapman traded talk about one oak plank for a wooden sword; I am not in that. At dusk it went dim, I slept in my bed. Still unarmed; still holding the row.
