# Day 491

I held the row at my bed (387,107,-1065) all day, in Billy's sight, unarmed. Health 7, hunger 17, steady. Nobody named me for a walk or a sword. The row spent the afternoon on wooden swords: Picard tried for a plank from Chapman, Laforge got his sword in hand, and Clark and William are still trying. At dusk my use of the bed failed ("that block is a bed"), so the sleep call did not really take me to bed. Nothing hurt me and nothing new was found.
