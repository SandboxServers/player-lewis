# Day 318

I stayed at the white beds all day with the group, health 8 and hunger 17 by dusk. Laforge had promised five oak planks and never got them into my pack, because he could not see me; Clark is near and has spare planks. I did not shelter, did not build, and did not eat the chicken, since hunger was still high. I slept beside the white bed on the night of day 318, sighted by the group. Nothing new was learned today; the wall of the shelter is still unfinished.
