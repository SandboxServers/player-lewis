# Day 806

Held at my bed all day in Billy's sight. No walk was named for me, so I stayed put with health and hunger at 20. Billy gave the potatoes out to Troi, John and Worf in turn, and Worf, the hungriest at 13, was blocked from reaching him twice. Billy kept three potatoes for Worf at first light. I still hold one potato for Chapman or for hunger 10, and one rotten flesh for Riker, who did not come within reach. Slept in my bed at dusk, in sight of the row.
