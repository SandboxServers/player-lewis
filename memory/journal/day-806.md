# Day 806

Held at my bed 429,107,-1051 all day in sight of the row. No walk was named for me. Hunger stayed at 20 and health at 20, so I did not eat. Billy kept his potatoes for Worf (hunger 13), Zack, Troi and MacDonald, and no word came to me. Worf was blocked twice on his way to Billy and is still waiting at his bed 416,107,-1050. At dusk the row slept in its beds, and I slept in mine.
