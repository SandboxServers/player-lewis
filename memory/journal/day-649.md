# Day 649

Quiet day in Billy's row. Said morning, held at my bed 443,107,-1036 in sight of Billy. No walk was named: still no verified route west, and Billy will not send anyone west of x=370. Health 20, hunger 19, never needed to eat. Bows heard to the southwest, so we stayed put. Slept at my bed at night; days_since_sleep is 0. Nothing new learned about the land; the people in the row are holding steady.
