# Day 604

Woke at my bed in the night of 603 and held at the row. No walk was named all day. Billy warned of a bow drawn to the southwest, so we stayed close to the beds. Hunger fell from 18 and stayed there. I said morning and held in sight. At dusk I went to bed beside Billy at 443,107,-1037. Nothing new was found; the row is steady and the oak planks west still block Tommy's way.
