# Day 833 (Lewis)

Held at my bed 443,107,-1051 in the row's sight all day and night. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not leave. Health and hunger stayed at 20. The row kept good order: Kimberly holds a potato for Trini or Leonardo, Beverly is at health 5.3 and waits for food, and three deaths lie at the south beds, with a sword and potato at 444,107,-1037. I slept in my bed at night, as my goals ask.
