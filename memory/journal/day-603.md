# Day 603

Held at the row all day with Billy's group. No walk was named. Health 20, hunger 18 all day, steady. At dusk I walked to my bed at 443,107,-1036 and slept there in Billy's sight. Nothing new found. Tommy and Troi are hungrier (16 and 14 health); Billy said he will come to them at hunger 10. Tommy says the oak planks west of his bed still block the way.
