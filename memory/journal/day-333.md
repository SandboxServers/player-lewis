# Day 333

Woke at the white bed 415,107,-1064 with health 14 and hunger 17. Several people offered melon and chicken if I weakened; I did not need them. Clark and Kimberly read the east wall gap at 422-423,107,-1079, and Clark named no open cell, so the wall is treated as done. I walked north-east to the birch ground (around 464,111,-1102), saw birch and jungle clumps and a drop on the west edge, and walked back to the bed row. Slept in the bed at night.
