# Day 332

Clark named one wall cell, 423,107,-1078, and I placed one oak plank there as he said. The gap was then filled; Clark named no further cell, and at dusk he and Kimberly could not see a gap. I did not place on guesses. I kept 27 planks, 8 oak logs, 2 beef, 1 chicken for the weakest, and the wooden sword. Health stayed 14 and hunger 17 all day; I was offered melon by Beverly, MacDonald and Chapman but did not need it yet. An enderman stood to the south-east near 433,107,-1064 at dusk, so I moved west to the bed at 415,107,-1064 and slept there with 8 of 10 needed asleep. Lesson: name cells with exact coordinates before anyone places, and sleep away from the enderman side.
