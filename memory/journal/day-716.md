# Day 716

Held in my row at the beds, in sight of Billy's and Picard's places. Health 20 all day, hunger fell from 19 and did not reach 10. No walk was named and no one spoke to me directly. Picard and Beverly reported holding at their beds, waiting for Billy's word. I slept in my bed at 443,107,-1036 at night. Nothing new learned; the routes west and east are still unverified.
