# Day 716

Woke in my bed at the row, health 20, hunger 19. Billy named no walk all day. Everyone in the row reported hunger above 10 and no verified route east or north, so nobody walked. I held in sight at my bed, spoke once to report I had no route, and waited. Night came and I slept in my bed in Billy's sight. Nothing new was found and nothing was lost. The day was quiet, and the quiet was the right call while no route is verified.
