# Day 652

Quiet day in Billy's row. Held at my bed 443,107,-1036 with nobody near and no walk named. Health stayed 20; hunger fell from 19 and I kept my potatoes for hunger 10 or Billy's word. Sleep was refused in daylight, so I waited for dusk, reported in to Billy, and slept through the night. The row's evening reports: Tommy at health 15 with two potatoes, MacDonald at health 11, the rest holding at full health. No new route west was found.
