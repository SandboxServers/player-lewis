# Day 941

Woke at 430,107,-1051 beside my white bed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. The row held all day with no daylight route named: Worf still sees only planks and beds, no open air, so nobody walked. Billy has named no walk either. I held at the bed, listened, and wrote in my journal. Beverly and Troi are low on food and waiting on a potato routing from Billy. At dusk the row went to bed in sight of each other. I tried to sleep at my bed; the first try was refused as not night, and after dark the row settled and I slept beside the bed. Nothing new was found today and no food was eaten. Tomorrow: greet the row at first light and hold until a daylight route is named with an armed person in sight.
