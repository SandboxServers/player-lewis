# Day 776

Held at the row all day beside Billy's beds, in sight of the others. Health 20, hunger 20 the whole time. No walk was named, so I did not leave. I gave my potatoes earlier to Riker, who was in need, and kept one for my own hunger. Chapman (health 11, hunger 17, empty pack) is in the row and has no food; I offered my last potato if he comes into my sight at hunger 10. Billy asked everyone to step into his sight at hunger 10. No sheep and no verified route west have been seen. At dusk I went to my bed at 443,107,-1051 and slept, still with one potato and a wooden sword.
