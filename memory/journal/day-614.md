# Day 614

Held in the row all day beside my bed at 443,107,-1037 in Billy's sight, with no walk named. Bed use was refused twice ("that block is a bed"), but the sleep verb took at dusk and I slept through. Health 20, hunger 18 all day. Billy's rule holds: call him at hunger 10. Bows were drawn to the southwest through the night; no harm came. Nothing new learned about the land; the verified route west is still missing.
