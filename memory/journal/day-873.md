# Day 873

Dusk came with phantoms near Kimberly and the row. Billy kept the potatoes moving: Worf 5 and Zack 3, handed out slowly because the row would not walk in the dark. I stayed unarmed at my bed, did not walk, and watched from the row. I went to my bed at 443,107,-1051 and called sleep; two of the ten needed were asleep at the last count. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Nothing new to act on until first light, when I greet the row and wait for Billy to name a walk.
