# Day 577

Held at the row in Billy's sight all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 18. Billy fed Tommy and walked to Zack's field, but never named a walk for me. Nothing was asked of me. At dusk Billy named Jason a bed at 429,107,-1037, which was blocked, so Jason held at the row. I slept in my bed at 443,107,-1037 in Billy's sight, with a handoff note written first.
