# Day 1102

Held in the row all day beside my white bed at 443,107,-1051. No routing came from Billy or Worf, and no food reached me. Trini still holds two potatoes for Worf and Zack, and they have not met to hand them over. Riker, Beverly and Deanna hold at their beds too, all waiting on Billy. Health 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. I said once that I walk nowhere until a route is named, and I did not break a plank or leave the row. Slept at dusk beside the bed.
