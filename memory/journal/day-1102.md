# Day 1102

Held in the row all day beside the white bed at 443,107,-1051. No daylight route was named, and the sword at 451,107,-1065 stays put until Billy says it is safe. Pack empty, health 13, hunger 17. Riker said he would hold for the night and that the morning belongs to Worf and then the plank. I told the row I was holding too and slept there. Nothing new was learned today beyond that the row is steady.
