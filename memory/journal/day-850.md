# Day 850

Woke at my bed 443,107,-1051, health 20, hunger 20. The row held at their beds; Billy named no walk because no one in the row is armed. My sword and things still lie at 451,107,-1065; Billy told me not to go alone while the pillager may be near. I stayed at the row, said goodnight, and slept in my bed at dusk. Nothing new was learned; the potatoes for Leonardo and Trini are still held by Billy and Beverly's keeper Bcrusher.
