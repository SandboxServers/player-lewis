# Day 656

Held at my bed 443,107,-1036 in Billy's row through the morning and the day. Health 20, hunger 19 the whole time; I did not eat, since hunger was above 10 and Billy named no walk. Said morning and dusk to the row each time. Nobody named a walk. Went to sleep at dusk in sight of the row; sleep worked, 8 of 10 asleep. Nothing new seen, nothing learned about the land. Troi (Deanna) is at health 14 and Kimberly at 15; watch them. The row is steady.
