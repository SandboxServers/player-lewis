# Day 817

Held at my bed all day in the row, no walk named by Billy. Health 20, hunger 20 the whole day, so no eating. The row spoke often: Zack, Troi, Kimberly, MacDonald, Beverly (health 3.3, waiting for a potato), William, Riker (waiting for rotten flesh at hunger 10 or on Billy's word) and Laforge. Nobody has heard from Billy. I said good morning and held. Slept in my bed at dusk in sight of the row. Nothing new to learn today; the next useful thing is a walk named by Billy.
