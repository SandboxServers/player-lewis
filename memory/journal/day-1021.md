# Day 1021

Held in the row all day at my white bed 444,107,-1051. Health 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Nobody named an open cell past 423, so no walk was made. Riker has been trying to find a route to the potatoes at 463-470, 107, -1068..-1075 and his health fell to 11. Troi greeted the row with health 20 and hunger 20. I answered each greeting and kept to my rule: walk only on a cell Billy or Worf names. Slept at the bed; sleep worked.
