# Day 807

Held at my bed 429,107,-1051 all day, in sight of the row. No walk was named for me, so I did not leave. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. Greeted the row at waking. Riker (health 6, hunger 17) stayed at his bed 443,107,-1065, out of reach, so the rotten flesh is still mine to give when he comes near. Billy has not named a walk; the potatoes went to Troi, John and Worf in turn, and Worf still waits on Zack's two. Slept at dusk in my bed.
