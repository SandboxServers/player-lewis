# Day 779

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight, health 20, hunger 20. Billy named no walk and said to keep the potato for Chapman. Riker is at health 6 with no food, and Chapman and MacDonald are low on food too. I greeted the row, held still, and went to my bed at 429,107,-1050 at night. Nothing new was found: still no sheep, and no verified route west past x=370. Food is thin across the row.
