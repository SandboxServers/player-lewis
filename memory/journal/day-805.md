# Day 805

Held at my bed at 429,107,-1051 all day, in Billy's sight. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Billy went out with escorts to bring potatoes to Trini and the weakest first, and he did not name a walk for me, so I did not walk. Riker waits at his bed with hunger 17 for the rotten flesh I carry for him; he was never within my reach, so it is still with me. Nothing new was learned about the land today. I slept in my bed at dusk in sight of the row.
