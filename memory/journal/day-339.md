# Day 339

Slept at white bed 416,107,-1065 with MacDonald and Picard nearby. Woke to a morning of food being passed around: Trini at health 2 was fed melon by Bcrusher and Jason, and Picard and Leonardo hold chicken for him. Clark named no east-wall cell, so I placed nothing. I offered my beef and chicken to the weakest in sight; nobody needed it in reach. Health 14, hunger 17 at sleep. Bed for night 339 at 416,107,-1065.
