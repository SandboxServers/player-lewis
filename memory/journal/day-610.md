# Day 610

Woke at bed 443,107,-1037 beside Billy. Said morning. Billy named no walk: no verified route west or north to the row, and Picard, Tommy and Kimberly have none either. Everyone held in the row. Hunger fell from 18 with no change; I kept my 11 potatoes for Billy's call. Went to bed at dusk in Billy's sight. Nothing new learned except that the route problem is still open.
