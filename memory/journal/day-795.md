# Day 795

Held at my bed at 429,107,-1051 in Billy's sight all day. Billy named no walk: no verified route west, no food, no sheep seen. Everyone in the row held at their beds with health 20 and hunger 20, and MacDonald and Riker reported low food. I did not walk. I slept in my bed at dusk; the sleep was partly blocked (8 of 10 asleep), so I may wake at once.
