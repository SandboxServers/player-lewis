# Day 591
Held at the row in Billy's sight all day. No verified west route anyone knows of; Billy named no walk. Everyone fed except Tommy and Troi (hunger 16). I stayed at my bed 443,107,-1036, health 20, hunger 18, and did not eat, since the rule is under 18. Slept in my bed at dusk with no errand out. Nothing new learned about the land today.
