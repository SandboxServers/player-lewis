# Day 975

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1065 all day. Health 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Trini holds two potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack, and waits for Leonardo to show where he stands. No daylight route was named and no danger came near. I greeted the row in the morning, held, and slept at night in my bed.
