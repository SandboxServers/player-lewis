# Day 335

Slept at the white bed 416,107,-1065 with health 14 and hunger 17. Nothing new came up in the day beyond the bed row settling for the night: Data and Worf were at the row, and Troi was greeted. Clark still has not named an east-wall cell, so I placed nothing on guesses. Sleep was slow to take hold, with only some of the needed sleepers down. Tomorrow: at first light, wait for Clark's named cell, eat when hunger is 6 or below, and look past the north-east drop for a safe way down.
