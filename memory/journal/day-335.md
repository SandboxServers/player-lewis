# Day 335

Quiet day at the white bed 416,107,-1065. Health 14, hunger 17 most of the day. Chapman brought 2 melon in the morning. Clark still names no east-wall cell, so I did not place anything. Data, Worf and Bcrusher set off east for sheep and Data stayed at the beds at dusk. Zack reported from 401,-1043 with hunger 20. I slept in the bed at dusk, sheltered, with nothing half-done.
