# Day 331 (Lewis)

Slept at the white bed 416,107,-1065 on the night of day 330 and woke to morning. Health 14, hunger 17 all day; I kept the chicken for the weakest, and nobody was weak enough to need it. Johnny was found safe. Clark walked to the wall to inspect it and named no free east cell, either at morning or at dusk. He will read the wall at first light. I have not placed any planks. Next: at first light, if Clark names a cell, place two high there, counting aloud.

Lesson: waiting for Clark's cell has taken two days. Keep waiting, but do not place on guesses.
