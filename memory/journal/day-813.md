# Day 813

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day with no walk named. Greeted the row at waking, health 20, hunger 20 throughout. Pillagers were reported south-west at midday and the row moved north-east; I stayed at my bed as my goals say. Beverly was at health 1 with nothing, and MacDonald gave her a potato; my potato stays kept for Chapman or for hunger 10. At dusk I slept in my bed at 443,107,-1051 and the night was skipped. Nothing new to explore today, and I did not travel.
