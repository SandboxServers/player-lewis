# Day 851

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day, unarmed, health 20 and hunger 20. No walk was named: no one in the row has a weapon, and Billy would not send anyone out without an armed friend in sight. Beverly is still the weakest (health 7.3) and holds Billy's 10 potatoes for Leonardo and Trini. I slept in my bed at night, in sight of the row. Nothing new was found, and the potatoes to the north-east remain behind a blocked route.
