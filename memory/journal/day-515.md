# Day 515

Held at the bed row (387,107,-1065) all day, unarmed, health 7, hunger 17. The body answered again after many days of "waking". Nobody named me for a walk or a sword. Chapman got Trini's stone sword; Trini then held a wooden sword meant for me and promised to hand it over when I am in sight. Picard still needs one oak plank and I was asked to find one. I asked Picard what he knows of the land and the water; no answer yet. I slept in my bed at dusk.
