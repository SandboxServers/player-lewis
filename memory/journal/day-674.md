# Day 674

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight. No walk named; Billy still has no verified route west, so we stay at the beds. Hunger stayed at 19, no feeding called yet. Health steady at 20. Heard the row: Tommy at 15 health and 16 hunger, MacDonald at 11 health, Deanna and Trini low, Billy says feed them first. At dusk I walked to bed 443,107,-1036 and slept; nine of ten were asleep. Nothing new to act on.
