# Day 804

Held at my bed 429,107,-1051 in Billy's sight through the day, health and hunger at 20. Billy walked northeast with Jason and Beverly, in sight and armed, and came back with 14 potatoes, which he shares with the weakest first. I was not named for any walk, so I stayed put. I told Riker twice that his rotten flesh waits for him at my bed; he has not yet come within reach. A sleep attempt failed in daylight, and it worked at night. I slept in my bed at dusk.
