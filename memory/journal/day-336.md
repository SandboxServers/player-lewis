# Day 336

Night at the white beds, 416,107,-1065. Health 14, hunger 17. Clark still named no east-wall cell, so I placed nothing on guesses. Zack reported an enderman near the beds and backed off north-west. I stayed at my bed, sword in pack, and slept. Still to do at first light: wait for Clark's named cell, eat when hunger is 6 or below, and look past the north-east drop for a safe way down.
