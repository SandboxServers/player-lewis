# Day 336

Quiet day at the white beds, 416,107,-1065. Health 14, hunger 17 all day, no food eaten. Clark named no east-wall cell, so I placed nothing on guesses. The sheep search east with Data, Worf, Chapman and Bcrusher was turned back: the way at 472,107 is blocked again. Tommy, Billy, Laforge and Picard are at the bed row; Picard and Bcrusher hold food for the weak. I slept in the bed at dusk with the group in sight.
