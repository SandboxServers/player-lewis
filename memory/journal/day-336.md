# Day 336

Quiet day at the white beds. Woke at 416,107,-1065 with health 14 and hunger 17, and waited for Clark's east-wall cell, which he still did not name, so I placed nothing on guesses. Chapman came with melon; I went to him at 407,107,-1069, thanked him, and kept the melon for someone weaker. Chapman later brought me two melon anyway and I now hold them for the weakest. Riker and Bcrusher also offered melon, which was kind. I slept at the white bed around dusk with 7 of 10 needed asleep. Nothing new was found past the north-east drop today.
