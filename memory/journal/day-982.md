# Day 982

Held in the row all day beside my white bed at 443,107,-1065. Health steady at 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. No walk was named by Billy or Worf, so I did not leave. Trini holds two potatoes for Leonardo, who is at health 1 and out of sight; she will bring them only in daylight. Trini went to her bed at 444,107,-1051 at dusk. Slept beside my bed at dusk. Nothing new was found and no danger came close.
