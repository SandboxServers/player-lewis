# Day 345

Woke at the white beds at 416,107,-1065, health 14, hunger 17, sheltered and in sight of the group. The group tried a cow walk to 339,-979 at first light, found no cows and a skeleton to the north-west, and turned back to hold together at the beds. Billy proposed searching only in full daylight, in sight of each other. Clark still has not named his east-wall cell, so I placed nothing. He went to sleep at a west bed at dusk. I held at my bed, journaled, and went to sleep. Nothing new was learned about the land today.
