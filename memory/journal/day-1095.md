Day 1095. Held in the row all day beside white bed 443,107,-1051. No daylight route was named by Billy, Worf or two armed readers, so I did not walk. Pack stayed empty, health 13, hunger 17. Trini still holds 2 potatoes for Worf, then Leonardo, then Zack, and a wall blocks her view of Worf. Riker, Troi, Beverly and Trini all held in the row and said goodnight. Nothing new was learned today except that the row is steady and waiting. Slept at dusk beside the bed.
