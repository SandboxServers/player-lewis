# Day 549

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight, hunger 17, health 7, unarmed. No cow walk: the pillagers at 370-380,-1022 still block the route, and Picard knows no way around. Billy says the first food goes to Lewis and Riker. I waited, wrote one line in the journal, and slept in my bed at 401,107,-1037 at dusk with the row around me. Nothing new was learned except that the row is holding and everyone is waiting on Billy's word.
