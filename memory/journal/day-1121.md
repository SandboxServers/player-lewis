# Day 1121

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1065 in the row all day, unarmed and empty-handed, health 10.3, hunger 17. No route past 423 was named by Billy or Worf. Riker, Trini and Chapman held with me and said they would walk only on a named route with two armed readers. Nobody had food to share. I said I was holding, then slept at dusk beside the bed.
