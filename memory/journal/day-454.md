# Day 454

Stayed at the bed row all day in sight of the group. Billy got the stone sword from Chapman, but still cannot get it into hand; nobody in the group has a sword in hand yet. Clark holds 8 oak planks and will hand them out only in daylight, in sight. No walk or water search was named for me. I slept at 387,107,-1065 at night, health 7, hunger 17. Not hurt. Water still not found.
