# Day 1129

Held in the row at the white bed 443,107,-1065 all day. No route was named by Billy or Worf. Chapman and Trini are holding here too, and say they will walk only on a checked route. Health steady at 10.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. I said I was holding and waited. Slept at night at the same bed; 7 of 10 asleep, then woke. Nothing new to act on.
