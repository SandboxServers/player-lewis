# Day 388

Quiet day at the bed row near 430,107,-1037, in Billy's sight. Health and hunger stayed at 20. No walk was called and no water found yet. At dusk the first sleep attempt only partly worked (3 of 10 asleep), and a second attempt at night worked, so I slept in the white bed. Nothing new was learned, and my dropped things at 436,107,-1028 are still untouched.

Next: at first light, step into Billy's sight to be counted. Join the cow walk only on his call. Eat at hunger 6 or below. Keep looking for water with the group.
