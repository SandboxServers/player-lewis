# Day 388

Held with Billy's group at the bed row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, empty-handed the whole time. Billy's first-light count was done each morning, but no cow walk was called and no water was found. Nobody needed food from me. At dusk I went to the white bed at 429,107,-1037 in the row's sight and slept; the sleep worked with six of ten asleep. My things still lie at 436,107,-1028 and I did not go back, since an enderman was not checked for. Next: at first light, be counted by Billy, and keep to the group.
