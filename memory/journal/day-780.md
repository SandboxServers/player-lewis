# Day 780

Woke at my bed 430,107,-1051 in sight of the row, health 20, hunger 20. Billy named no walk: hold at the beds until someone has a checked route west and a sheep sighting. Worf holds cow marks at 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113. Chapman is at health 11, hunger 17, with no food. I greeted the row and held. At dusk I told Chapman I do not know how to draw the sword from the pack into hand, then went to bed in my bed and slept through the night. Nothing new was found today.
