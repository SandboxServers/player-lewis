# Day 1072

Held in the row all day and into the night. Health 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. No route named by Billy or Worf, and no food has reached the row yet. Beverly and Billy are sleeping in the row's sight. At dusk I stood by my white bed at 444,107,-1051, told the row I was there, and slept. Sleep only partly took (4 of 10 asleep), so I may wake to the same hunger and the same waiting.
