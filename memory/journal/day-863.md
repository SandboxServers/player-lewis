Day 863. Held at my bed in the row all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed. Billy handed potatoes to Worf and Zack in daylight, in sight, and was owed 5 and 3 more at first light. No walk was named for me, so I did not leave the row. Slept at bed 443,107,-1051 in sight of the row. Nothing new was found; the blank places on my map stay blank for now.
