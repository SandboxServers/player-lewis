# Day 963

Held in the row all day at my white bed 430,107,-1051. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No daylight route was named by Billy or Worf, and the row is still walled by planks with no open air seen. Nobody came near in the night. The first sleep got only 2 of 10 asleep; the second sleep worked and I rested through the night. Nothing new was learned today. Next: greet the row at first light and hold until a route is named.
