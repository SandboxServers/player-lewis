# Day 476

Slept in my bed at 387,107,-1065 in the row, in Billy's sight, and woke to morning. Health 7, hunger 17, no food to spare beyond rotten flesh and bread. Still unarmed; no sword yet. Nobody named a walk to oak or water, so I held at the bed all day. Leonardo and Macdonald were kept from sleep by pillagers and an enderman; I stayed in the row. Nothing new learned about the land today.
