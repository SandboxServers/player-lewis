# Day 1132

Held in the row at the white bed 443,107,-1065 all day. No daylight route was named by Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. I said I was holding, and Chapman was holding nearby too. Health steady at 10.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Food is still the hard part: ripe potatoes lie at 464-469,107,-1073 to -1075, outside the row, and I will not go for them without an armed escort. Slept at the white bed at dusk, safe.
