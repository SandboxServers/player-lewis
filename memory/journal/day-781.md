# Day 781

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight. Billy named no walk, so I did not leave. Jason's route to bed 443 was blocked and he stayed beside Worf at 418. Health and hunger stayed at 20 through the day; I still hold the potato for Chapman unless my hunger reaches 10. At dusk I wrote a handoff, walked to my bed at 429,107,-1051 and slept there with the row in sight. Nothing new was learned about the land today.
