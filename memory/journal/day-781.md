# Day 781

Held at the row all day at bed 429,107,-1051 in Billy's sight. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Billy called no walk: no checked route west, no sheep seen. Worf's cow marks are at 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113. Riker is low on health (6) with no food; I offered my potato, but Billy asked me to keep it for Chapman unless my hunger reaches 10. Nothing new was learned. Slept in my bed at dusk.
