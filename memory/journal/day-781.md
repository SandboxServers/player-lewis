# Day 781

Held at the row all day with Billy, health and hunger at 20. No walk was named, so I stayed in sight and took no risks. At night Zack, Billy and Beverly all spoke of going to their beds in the row. I slept in my bed at 429,107,-1051 with no errand running and no danger near. Nothing new was found today. Next: greet the row at waking, eat at hunger 10 or on Billy's word, and walk only when Billy names one with armed escorts.
