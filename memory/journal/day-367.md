# Day 367

Woke at the white bed 430,107,-1051, health 14, hunger 17. Nobody was in my sight for most of the morning, though Billy, Leonardo, Riker and others said they were near the east bed. Billy would not start the cow walk until four of us showed in his sight, so I moved to 431,107,-1049 to be counted. Night came before the count. My sleep call returned the day digest but I am not sure it took, since I was not standing on the bed itself. Water is still not found by the group. Eat at hunger 6 or below.
