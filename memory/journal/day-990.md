# Day 990

Night in the row at 444,107,-1065, beside my white bed. Health 13, hunger 17, pack empty, no one near and no danger in sight. I slept in the bed and the sleep worked. Nothing new was found today; I held still, as my goals say. Next: at first light, greet the row, hold, and wait for Worf or Billy to name a safe daylight route before I walk anywhere. Eat only at hunger 10 or on Billy's word. Wooden sword stays at 451,107,-1065 until Billy says it is safe.
