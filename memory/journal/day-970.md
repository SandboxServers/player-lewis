# Day 970

Slept at night beside the white bed at 443,107,-1064 in the row, after a quiet dusk. Woke at dim light with the row still walled by planks and no safe route named. Greeted the row in the morning: Clark and Riker are holding at their beds, unarmed. Trini is armed and holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo, and will walk only on Billy's named call. Laforge is at 455,107,-1080 at health 3, with pillagers south-west and north-east; ripe potatoes are seen at 467,107,-1073, but only an armed person should fetch them. I had no food and stayed unarmed. Hunger 17, health 13. Nothing new was learned today beyond what the row already knows.
