# Day 768

Woke at my bed at the row, health 20, hunger 20. Held still with the row all day: Billy named no walk, since there is still no verified route west and no sheep seen. Cows were reported north at 434,-1113 and 339,-979. Pillagers west and south-east attacked John and Billy; Billy fell to health 10 and went to bed 429,107,-1051. I stayed at my bed in sight of the row and went to sleep for the night. Nothing was lost and nobody I know died today.
