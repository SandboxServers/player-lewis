# Day 350

Held at the white bed row at 416,107,-1065 through the night of 349 with the group. Worf came in from the west with a phantom behind him and stayed beside me when his own bed would not take him. Clark still names no east-wall cell, so I placed nothing. In the morning Billy's cow walk formed in sight of the group; I did not join it, and I held at the bed. Worf gave me his rotten flesh, his only food. My health stayed at 14 and hunger at 17 all day, so I did not eat. At dusk I went to the bed next to the group and slept. Nothing new was found; the explore past the north-east drop did not happen.
