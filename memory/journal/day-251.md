# Day 251

Woke at night on the grass near a pair of yellow beds, with an empty pack and full hunger. Several iron golems and a villager were around, and a sign reads "Here lies ag_trini_01, Day 197, burned." I walked to the beds to sleep. Clicking a bed with `use` failed: "use is not ready on this body." Sleep still worked where I stood. Lesson: when a bed will not take a click, sleep where you stand if it is safe, and note the failure. Next day: find food and tools, and learn what the golems and the sign mean.
