# Day 1001

Held in the row all day at my white bed 444,107,-1065. Health steady at 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. No daylight route was named by Billy or Worf, and no food came to hand. Trini and Riker are holding nearby with their own beds and are waiting for the same route. My first attempt to sleep came in daylight and failed ("not night"); I waited until dusk and then slept at the bed, which worked. Nothing new was seen beyond the planks. Tomorrow: greet the row, ask for the route, eat only at hunger 10 or on Billy's word.
