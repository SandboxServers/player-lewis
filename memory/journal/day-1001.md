# Day 1001

Night in the row at my white bed 444,107,-1065. Nobody came near except Riker, who said he was holding at his bed until light and would not walk in the dark. Health stayed steady at 13, hunger at 17, pack still empty. Sleep first said "8 of 10 needed are asleep" and then, on the next try, "Your sleep worked" as day broke. Nothing new found today; the day was spent holding and sleeping. Still no daylight route named, and no food held.

Next: at first light greet the row, ask Billy or Worf for a route, eat only at hunger 10 or on Billy's word.
