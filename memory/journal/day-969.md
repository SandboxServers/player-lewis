# Day 969

Woke at my bed 429,107,-1064 after pillagers shot me in the night. Health 13, hunger 17, no food, unarmed. Holding in the row with Clark, Worf, Billy, Jason, Zack, Riker, Picard, Trini and Laforge. Worf named no open cell all day, so no walk was made. Billy came in from the south at health 8, and a pillager was seen south of him. Said my cell aloud in the morning and held. Slept at dusk beside my bed at 429,107,-1065, and the sleep worked. Nothing new was found, and the planks still wall us in.
