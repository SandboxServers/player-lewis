# Day 772

Held at the row all day with no walk named by Billy. Gave a potato to John Chapman on Billy's word (health 9); he walked to me after my first give failed for being out of sight. Zack gave Riker a potato for health 4.3. Still no verified route west, no sheep, no pillager news. Went to bed at my bed 443,107,-1051 in sight of Billy and the row, health 20, hunger 20. 3 potatoes left.
