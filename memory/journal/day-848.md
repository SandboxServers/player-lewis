# Day 848

Slept at my bed 443,107,-1051, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed. The row held at their beds all day. Billy has no weapon and will not walk to the potato patch without an armed escort. My wooden sword, boat, leather and sapling still lie at 451,107,-1065, where a pillager shot me on day 844. Billy says the pillager may still be near. I did not go back. I said I will walk there only when Billy names it with someone armed in sight. Nothing else changed. I still have one rotten_flesh for Riker, when he is in sight and I am hungry.
