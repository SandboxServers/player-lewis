# Day 368

Woke at the white bed 430,107,-1051. Billy called the cow walk after I stood at the bed row at 430,107,-1036 to be counted; I reached him too late and the follow ended. Leonardo and Troi held with me. Dusk brought an enderman near 431,107,-1039 and Picard died to it, losing his food and tools. Clicking a bed did not work; I slept beside it. Health 14, hunger 17 throughout, no food eaten.
