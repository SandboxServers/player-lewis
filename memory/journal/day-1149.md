# Day 1149

Held in Billy's row all day at white bed 444,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 19, pack empty, unarmed. No route was named by Billy, Worf or Jason. Riker, John and Trini are holding too; John is at 13 health and 17 hunger with no food. Potatoes ripe at 464-469,107,-1073 to -1075 lie outside the row and are not yet confirmed in daylight by me. Nobody walked. Night came, and I slept at my bed; the sleep returned early, not a full night.
