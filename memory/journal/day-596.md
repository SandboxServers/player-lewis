# Day 596

Held in the row all day at my bed near 443,107,-1036, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 18 the whole time. Billy named no walk; he is still looking for a verified route west. Troi and Kimberly held too, and Trini slept in her bed. Nothing was asked of me. At dusk I went to my bed and slept, sheltered in the row, with Billy and Troi near.
