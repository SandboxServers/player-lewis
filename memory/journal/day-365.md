# Day 365

Quiet day at the white beds, 430,107,-1051. Health 14, hunger 17 throughout. Wesley and Picard are at the beds with their food held for the lowest. Billy's cow walk has not been called yet; it waits until four are in sight. I said I am ready to join. Slept at the bed in sight of the group. Water is still not found. Nothing new to explore today.
