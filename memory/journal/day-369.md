# Day 369

Woke at the white bed 430,107,-1037 with health 14 and hunger 17, still in the dim night. Riker and Tommy spoke of the cow walk and of first light at the pit; Billy has not yet called it. My rest intent failed on its precondition, so I went to sleep in the bed instead. Leonardo is nearby with a chicken for the lowest. Nothing new was found today. Water is still unknown. Tomorrow: stand where Billy can see me and ask to be counted for the cow walk; eat beef or chicken once hunger is 6 or below.
