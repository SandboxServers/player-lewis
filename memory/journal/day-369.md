# Day 369

Woke at the white bed 430,107,-1037. Said twice that I was there for Billy's cow walk. The intent I set to stand there ran out without anyone calling the walk. Riker, Tommy, Leonardo, and Troi (Deanna) all gathered at the beds and are ready when Billy calls. Health 14, hunger 17, no food needed yet. Went to sleep at the bed at dusk. Nothing new found today; water still unknown.
