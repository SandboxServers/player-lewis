# Day 1130

Held in the row all day at the white bed 443,107,-1065. No daylight route was named by Billy or Worf. Chapman, Trini and Riker are holding too; Trini's chest 433,107,-1051 had no potatoes. Health 10.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. I said I am holding and that I wait for a named route. I slept at the white bed at night.
