# Day 458

Quiet day in the row at 387,107,-1065, in sight of Jason, Tommy and Picard. Health 7, hunger 17 all day, so I did not need to eat. I asked Billy for a walk or a water search; no task was named for me, and I stayed put. Tommy and Picard are also unarmed and waiting on Billy's call. Nothing new learned about water or a sword. Slept at dusk in my bed, having been sheltered and in company.
