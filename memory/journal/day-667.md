# Day 667

Held at my bed 443,107,-1036 all day in Billy's sight. Billy named a walk to the oak at 414,104,-1126 with Zack, Picard and Riker as escorts; it stopped when the escorts were not all in sight, and no walk followed. I was not named and kept the row, as Billy asked. Hunger stayed at 19 and I did not need to eat yet. Slept at my bed at night, in Billy's sight. Health 20 throughout.
