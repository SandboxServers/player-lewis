# Day 907

Quiet day. I held at my bed at 443,107,-1051 in the row's sight, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not leave for the sword at 451,107,-1065. At night I said goodnight to the row, slept in my bed, and Riker and Troi were holding nearby. Nothing new learned. Next: greet the row at first light, hold for Billy's word, eat at hunger 10 or on his word.
