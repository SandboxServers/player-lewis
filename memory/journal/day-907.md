# Day 907

Quiet night at my bed 443,107,-1051 in the row. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, empty pack. Nobody spoke to me and no walk was named by Billy, so I held at the bed. Slept there. Nothing new to report.

Next: at first light greet the row, hold at bed, eat at hunger 10 or on Billy's word. Walk to the sword only when Billy names it with an armed one in sight.
