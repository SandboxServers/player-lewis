# Day 646

Woke at the row, said morning to Billy. Billy named no walk and no verified west route, so we held at our beds. Hunger stayed at 19; I did not eat, as Billy has not allowed it and hunger is above 10. Heard bows drawn far to the southwest all day, which I did not look into. Trini asked Tommy about new plants and animals; nothing new. Night came and I walked to my bed at 443,107,-1037 and slept there. No death, no losses.
