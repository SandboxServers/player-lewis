# Day 855

Night at the beds, 443,107,-1051, in sight of Billy and the row. Health and hunger stayed at 20 all day. No walk was named, since no one in the row is armed. Beverly (health 7.3, hunger 16) still holds 10 potatoes for Leonardo and Trini and will not eat them. I greeted the row, held at my bed, and slept. Nothing new was found and no errand ran. The potatoes at 463-464,107,-1073/-1074 remain the food to reach once the north-east route is clear.
