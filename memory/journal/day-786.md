# Day 786

Held at the row all day at my bed 429,107,-1051, in Billy's sight. No walk named. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. Billy asked the row for news of a route west, sheep or pillagers; I had none, and so said. Picard, Laforge, Jason and Riker reported the same: no route, no sheep, no pillager news. Chapman asked how to get a sword into hand. Nothing new was found. Slept in my bed at dusk.
