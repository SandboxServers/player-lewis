# Day 849

Held at my bed at 443,107,-1051 all day with the row. Unarmed (my wooden sword lies at 451,107,-1065), no food, health 20, hunger 20. Billy named no walk: no one in the row was armed, so none was called. Potatoes (10 each held by Billy and Bcrusher) are set aside for Leonardo and Trini. Beverly is at health 7.3 and hunger 16, and Bcrusher holds her potatoes. Nothing new was found and no one was hurt. Slept at my bed at dusk.
