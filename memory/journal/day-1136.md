# Day 1136

Held in the row all day at the white bed 443,107,-1065. No route named by Billy or Worf. Riker, Chapman and Trini also hold here with empty packs; the chest by the beds was checked, no food found. Hunger stayed at 17 and I ate nothing, as my rule says. Health steady at about 10. Thunder in the afternoon, then night. Slept at the white bed as usual.
