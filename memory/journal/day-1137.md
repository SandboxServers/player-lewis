# Day 1137

Held at the white bed 444,107,-1065 in Billy's row all day. Unarmed, pack empty, hunger 17, health steady at 10.3. Said I am holding. Trini, Riker and Chapman are holding too and report no food and no weapon. No daylight route was named by Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. Slept at the bed at night; nothing new came of it.
