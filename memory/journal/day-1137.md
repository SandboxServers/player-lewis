# Day 1137

Held at the white bed at 444,107,-1065 in Billy's row all day, unarmed, pack empty, hunger 17, health steady near 10. Riker, Trini, Chapman and William also held; nobody named a checked daylight route, so nobody walked. I said I was holding and waited through the day. Nothing new was learned about the land. Went to sleep at night beside the white bed. Still waiting on Billy or Worf for a route and for food.
