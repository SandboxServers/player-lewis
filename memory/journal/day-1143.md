# Day 1143

Held all day in Billy's row beside the white bed at 444,107,-1065. Health steady near 10.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. No one came in sight. I said I was holding and waited. Trini and William (Riker) also reported holding; neither named a daylight route. I did not walk or eat, per my rules. At night I slept at the white bed. Nothing new was learned today beyond the potato field at 464-469,107,-1073 to -1075, which I have seen from the row but not yet confirmed in daylight.
