# Day 972

Held in the row all day and into the night. No food, no daylight route named by Worf or Billy, so I stayed beside my white bed at 443,107,-1065. Riker and Trini greeted the row and held too. I greeted the row and said I was holding. Slept at the bed; health 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Nothing new was learned; the walls are still planks.
