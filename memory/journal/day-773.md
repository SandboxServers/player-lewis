# Day 773

Held at the row all day in sight of Billy, Beverly, Trini, Troi and MacDonald. No walk was named, so I did not leave. Bows were drawn now and then to the southwest, never near. Greeted the row in the morning and told Troi I was holding. At dusk I went to the bed at 443,107,-1022 and slept. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. Troi has gone too long without sleep and was trying to lie down in the row. Nothing new about the land or the route west.
