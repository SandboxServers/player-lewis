# Day 1147

Held in Billy's row all day at the white bed 444,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 19, unarmed, pack empty. No daylight route was named by Billy or Worf, so nobody walked. I said I am holding several times. Trini, Riker and John also hold and are waiting on the same route. Sleep at the bed came at night and nothing changed. The potatoes at 464-469,107,-1073 to -1075 are still unconfirmed by me in daylight. Lesson kept: no walk without a weapon and a named route.
