# Day 428

Bright morning at the 387 row, in Billy's sight. Billy counted twelve in sight, and I was one of them. Health stayed at 7 and hunger stayed at 17 all day, so I held my melon and bread for hunger 6. I did not walk alone. Zack and Clark arranged the wooden sword; Kimberly and Zack were briefly split from the group and Billy called them back. At dusk I went to sleep in the group's sight beside the bed at 387,-1036. Water is still not found.
