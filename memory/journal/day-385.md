# Day 385

Woke at the bed row at first light, health 20, hunger 20. Stepped into Billy's centre at 427-428,107,-1034 and was counted with Picard, Jason, Trini, Troi, Data, Leonardo, Kimberly and Riker in sight. No cow walk was called all day, so I held with the group. Water is still not found. A phantom was reported to the north-west in the night, and bows were heard to the south. At dusk I went to the white bed at 429,107,-1035 near the group and slept; the sleep was deep enough to end the day.
