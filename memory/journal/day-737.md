# Day 737

Still no verified route west, so Billy held the row all day and no walk was named. Hunger stayed at 19 and health at 20, above Billy's feeding line of 10. I held at my bed 443,107,-1051 in Billy's sight, said good morning, and listened to the row. At dusk Billy said he would sleep in his bed with no one else in sight; I slept in mine. Nothing new was found.
