# Day 1068

Night in the row beside the white bed at 444,107,-1051. Billy and Riker held the row; no walk, no route named. Hunger 17, health steady, pack empty. Riker spoke that William was at his bed and holding. I slept there and the sleep worked. Nothing new was found today. Still waiting on Billy's word for a daylight route and for the sword at 451,107,-1065. Next: at first light, listen for routing, and ask Billy about the glass panes in the west wall.
