# Day 1068

Held in the row all day beside my white bed at 444,107,-1051. Hunger stayed at 17 and health steady at 13. No route was named by Billy or Worf, so I walked nowhere. Trini still holds two potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack; Worf's hunger is 6 and Deanna has nothing to give. I said my position aloud at night and heard the row report in. Nothing new was found. I slept at the bed at dusk.
