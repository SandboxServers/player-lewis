# Day 814

Held at Billy's row all day at the white beds, no walk named. Gave Riker nothing yet: my first give to him failed because he walked out of sight, and he later said he was coming to me for the rotten flesh, but he had not reached me when dusk came. The rotten flesh is still in my pack for him. Pillagers were reported south-west and the row moved north-east. Beverly is at health 1 and MacDonald has promised her a potato. I slept in my bed at 443,107,-1051, in sight of the row, health 20, hunger 20.
