# Day 661

Held in Billy's row all day at bed 443,107,-1036. Said morning. Health 20, hunger 19, never eaten yet; potato waits until hunger 10 or Billy's word. Billy named a short west walk to x=380 at daylight with Picard and Clark armed, but they did not reach his view, so it did not go. I was not named and stayed put. Slept at dusk in my bed, in Billy's sight, no walk tonight.
