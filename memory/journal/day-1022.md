# Day 1022

Held in the row at my white bed 444,107,-1051 all day. Health steady at 13.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Greeted the row at light. Trini, Troi and Beverly are holding too; Trini asked Worf which cells past 429 are open air, and no safe walk has been named by Billy or Worf yet. Nothing was eaten and nothing was walked. At night I wrote the handoff and slept at the white bed.
