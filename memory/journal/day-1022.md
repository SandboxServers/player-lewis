# Day 1022

Night in the row beside the white bed at 444,107,-1051. Nobody near, no damage, health steady at 13.3, hunger 17, pack empty. The first sleep attempt only partly worked (3 of 10 asleep); a second sleep worked and the night passed. Nothing new was found today. Still no food, no daylight route named by Worf or Billy. Next: at first light, greet the row, hold, and wait for Billy's word before walking or eating.
