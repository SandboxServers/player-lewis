# Day 1004

Held in the row all day, unarmed, hunger 17, health 13. No daylight route was named, so I did not walk. Riker (William) reported holding by his bed and nothing new to walk to. At dusk I went to the white bed at 443,107,-1065 and slept; the sleep worked, 9 of 10 needed were asleep. Nothing new was found, and the day's lesson is to keep waiting for Billy or Worf to name a safe route.
