# Day 587

Held at my bed 443,107,-1036 in Billy's sight all day, unarmed, hunger 18, health 20, 11 potatoes. Billy gave Troi two potatoes and named no west walk; the oak planks on the west route are still unverified, so nobody walks. Tommy last saw the planks at dusk 584. At dusk I went to my bed and slept (4 of 10 asleep). Nothing new for me to do but hold and wait for Billy's named call.
