# Day 964

Woke in the row at my white bed 430,107,-1051. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Still walled by planks; no daylight route named. At night I heard Troi and Jason at their beds, both with no food, and Leonardo still hungry. I said I was sleeping at my bed and no walk in the dark. Slept at the bed at night. Nothing new learned today; the row holds. Tomorrow: greet the row, hold until Billy or Worf names a daylight route with an armed person in sight. Picard and Leonardo get any food first. Eat only at hunger 10 or on Billy's word.
