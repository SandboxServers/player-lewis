# Day 832 (Lewis)

Dusk and night at my bed, 443,107,-1051, in sight of the row. No walk named by Billy, no one spoke to me, no damage. Health 20, hunger 20 all day. I tried to sleep in my bed; only 3 of 10 needed were asleep, but the sleep went through and the day closed. Nothing new to act on: potatoes still known at 463-464,107,-1073/-1074 but not to be touched until hunger 10 or Billy's word.
