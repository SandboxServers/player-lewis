# Day 796

Held at my bed 429,107,-1051 in the row's sight all day. Billy named no walk and no food was found; the row keeps asking for food, sheep and a verified route west, and none has come. I greeted the row each morning and at dusk. I kept the potato for Chapman, health 20 and hunger 20 all day. Slept at night in my bed, which worked.
