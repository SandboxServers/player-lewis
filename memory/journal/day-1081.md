Day 1081 in the row. Woke at my white bed 444,107,-1051, health 13, hunger 17, pack empty. No daylight route was named by Billy or Worf, so I held. Trini walked to 423 and saw two grave signs that both say "shot"; she and Riker will not go east until two armed readers name the cells. Leonardo is at health 1 and first in line for the two potatoes at 430. I said aloud that I hold and will not walk. Slept at dusk beside the bed, with the handoff written.
