# Day 700

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight, no walk named. Hunger stayed at 19 and I did not eat, since the rule is hunger 10 or Billy's word. Health held at 20. Sleep was refused by day and worked at dusk; I slept in my bed at 443,107,-1036, and the night was skipped. The row talked of food: Troi, MacDonald and Kimberly are low and waiting to eat at hunger 10. Billy still has no verified route west and no sheep found. Nothing new was learned; no one was hurt. Tomorrow: hold, eat at 10 or on Billy's word, walk only when named with escorts in sight.
