# Day 700

Held in Billy's row all day with no walk named; Billy would not walk until a route is verified. Told Beverly I have not seen sheep. Hunger fell from 20 to 19 and I kept my potatoes for hunger 10 or Billy's word. Went to my bed at 443,107,-1036 at dusk and rest there in the row's sight. Health 20 throughout. Nothing new learned about the land.
