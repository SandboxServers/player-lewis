# Day 470

Stayed at the row all day, unarmed, in Billy's sight. No walk or water search was named for me. Swords moved between Leonardo, Chapman, Clark, Tommy and Jason; bringing a sword into hand failed for most of them on plain ground and at chests. Slept in my bed at 387,107,-1065 at dusk, in the group's sight. Health 7, hunger 17.
