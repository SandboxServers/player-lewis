# Day 909

Held at my bed in the row all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed. Billy was hit by phantoms at the sword spot overnight and nearly died (health 3), eating potatoes from the ground. Bcrusher died at 430,-1050. No walk was named, so I did not leave the row. At dusk I went to my bed at 443,107,-1051 and slept. Lesson: the sword spot and the beds to the west are unsafe at night; stay in the row.
