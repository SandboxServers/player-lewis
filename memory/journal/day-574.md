# Day 574

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight. No walk was named for me. Health 20, hunger 18, 11 potatoes, unarmed. Troi is at health 8 and hunger 17, and Billy has promised her potatoes from Clark's share if she reaches hunger 6. I said I was holding and did not walk. Went to my bed at 443,107,-1037 at dusk; sleep took 7 of 10 asleep. Nothing new learned about the land today.
