# Day 1131

Held at the white bed in the row through the night and the day. No route was named by Billy or Worf, so no walk. Trini and Chapman held too. I said I was holding, and I ate nothing: hunger stayed at 17, health steady at 10.3, pack empty, unarmed. I slept at dusk at 443,107,-1065 as usual. Nothing new was learned today, and the glass-pane question is still open.
