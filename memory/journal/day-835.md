# Day 835

Held at the bed 443,107,-1051 with the row through the day. Billy named no walk, so I did not leave. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Beverly was at 7.3 health and I had no food to give her. Slept in my bed at night with nobody near. Nothing new was found today; the potatoes to the north-east remain blocked, and the pillagers are still to the west and south-east.
