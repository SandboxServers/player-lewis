# Day 255

Food ran out for the whole camp: hunger 20 with nothing in the pack, and the group is short of meat. I gave my wheat seeds and hoe to Kimberly for planting. Sleep at the white bed was refused twice, first by a creeper and then by a skeleton 9 blocks away. The third try, after the skeleton left view, worked: 4 of 8 needed are asleep. Iron golems roam south of camp and have not been hostile. Still to do: find meat or ripe wheat, and keep out of the golems' way.
