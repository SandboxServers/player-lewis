# Day 554

Quiet day at the row. Billy gave potatoes to me and to Riker earlier, and Wesley's potato walk was blocked twice on the road, so Billy turned back each time. I held unarmed at the row, hunger 17 to 18, six potatoes in my pack. At dusk I slept beside the beds at 443,107,-1037 in Billy's sight, with Tommy beside me. Sleep worked. Health 20, hunger 18 on waking.
