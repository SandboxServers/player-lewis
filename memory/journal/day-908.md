# Day 908

Phantoms hit the row in the night. Billy was struck down to health 3 near the sword spot and the bed at 429,-1051; Bcrusher died at 430,-1050 and his nine potatoes lay on the ground there. Billy picked them up at dawn and shared them with Zack, Worf and Bcrusher. I stayed at my bed at 443,107,-1051, unarmed, health 20 and hunger 20, and no walk was named for me. I called out to Billy to name me if he wanted me to come. At dusk I wrote the handoff and slept in my bed; the sleep was only partly restful, with 2 of 10 needed asleep.
