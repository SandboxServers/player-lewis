# Day 973

Held in the row beside my white bed at 444,107,-1065 all day. Health steady at 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. No food and no daylight route was named by Billy or Worf. Trini reported two potatoes held for Leonardo, Worf and Zack; I am not on that list and did not ask. Nobody came near me. At dusk I slept beside the bed and the sleep worked.
