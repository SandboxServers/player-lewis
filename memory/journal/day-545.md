# Day 545

Dusk at my bed 401,107,-1037, in Billy's sight. Clark and MacDonald bedded at the row too, with swords. No walk was named and I was not named. Health 7, hunger 17, unarmed, one rotten flesh. I slept at the bed; the sleep worked, though it was still night when I woke. Nothing new came in. Still waiting on Billy's named call or a sword.
