# Day 545

Woke at the bed row 401,107,-1037, health 7, hunger 17. Asked the people near about the trader and the land past the ridge; Chapman has not been past the ridge and does not know where the trader is. Billy held the row all day with Picard, Jason, Worf and MacDonald armed with wooden swords; no walk was named, and I was not named. I stayed in Billy's sight, unarmed. At dusk everyone went to their beds and I slept in mine. Nothing new to map today.
