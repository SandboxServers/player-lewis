# Day 838

Went with Billy in daylight to the east beds at 446,-1065, sword in hand, and walked the potatoes to Beverly's bed. Billy gave the weakest first; Bcrusher had 12 potatoes and held them for hunger 6. No one was attacked. I held close to Billy and did not wander. Back at my bed at night, health 20, hunger 20. Slept in my bed in the row's sight.
