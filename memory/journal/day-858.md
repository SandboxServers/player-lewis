# Day 858

Held at my bed at 443,107,-1051 all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed. Billy holds 10 potatoes for Leonardo and Trini and will bring them when Beverly or Worf is in sight. Beverly (health 7.3, hunger 16) and Worf (ate his potato at hunger 10, now without food) are the weakest. No armed friend came and no walk was named. I told Billy I am unarmed and staying put; he agreed and waits at the row. Nothing new was found today. Slept in my bed at night in sight of the row.
