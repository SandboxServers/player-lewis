# Day 399

Stayed at the bed row at 430,107,-1037 with the group in sight all day. Billy's walk call never came; he held at 430,107,-1023 with the others. No water found yet. Health and hunger stayed at 20 and I ate nothing. At dusk I tried to use the bed by act and it failed (a bed cannot be used that way), so I slept with the sleep call instead. Phantoms were attacking people in the open at dusk; Trini was hit down to 8.
