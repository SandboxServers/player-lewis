# Day 485

Held the bed row all day, unarmed, in Billy's sight, hunger 17, health 7 and steady. No walk was named. Leonardo reported Billy is now armed, and Picard's wooden sword went into Billy's pack. I said at the row that I will stand where Billy can see me for a give or walk. Still unknown: the exact steps Billy used to get a sword into hand. At dusk I went to my bed at 387,107,-1065 and began sleep; 5 of 10 needed were asleep at the last wait.
