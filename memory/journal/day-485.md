# Day 485

Quiet day at the row. I slept in my bed at 387,107,-1065 in Billy's sight, woke at night with health 7 and hunger 17, and stayed put. Nothing new about the sword: Billy is armed, but nobody has yet told me the exact steps to get one into hand. Zack's and Leonardo's gives landed in packs, not hands. Water (466,106,-1086) and oak (414,104,-1126) are still only noted, not checked. My dropped things at 436,107,-1028 remain unrecovered. Johnny holds 10 rotten flesh for when my hunger reaches 6.
