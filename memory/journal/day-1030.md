# Day 1030

Held in the row all day at my white bed 444,107,-1051. Health 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. No route or food named by Billy or Worf. Trini reached the potatoes' side; planks 424-429 still block the way to 423, and she would break them only with Billy or Worf watching. Riker is waiting for a seen route; Beverly and Troi are holding too. I did not walk. Slept at the bed at dusk. Nothing new learned, but the row is steady.
