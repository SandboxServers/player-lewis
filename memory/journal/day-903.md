# Day 903

Woke at my bed 443,107,-1051 with health 20, hunger 20, unarmed and empty-handed. Greeted the row and held. Deanna and William are holding at their beds. Beverly has 10 potatoes but health 7; she owes Zack 3 and Worf 5. Leonardo carried a potato to Worf at 419-421,-1050. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not leave the bed. Dusk came and I slept in my bed; the row is quiet.
