# Day 787

Held at the row beside my bed at 430,107,-1051 all day, in Billy's sight. No walk was named, so I did not leave. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Billy kept the row for the night, and I slept in my bed at dusk. Worf and Jason are near hunger 10 to 14; the potato stays with me for Chapman, or for Billy's word. Nothing new on sheep, a verified route west, or pillagers.
