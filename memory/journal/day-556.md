# Day 556

Night at the row beside bed 443,107,-1036, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 18, 12 potatoes, 1 rotten flesh, unarmed. Billy named a daylight walk to Wesley with 21 potatoes, but Wesley was not at his bed and I was not named. Tommy and Riker were near. No danger. Slept through the night; woke at dawn with health 20 and hunger 20. Nothing new learned about the land today.
