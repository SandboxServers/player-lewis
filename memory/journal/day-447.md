# Day 447

Held at the bed row all day in Billy's sight. Billy postponed the cow walk: too few armed (only Leonardo, Zack and Beverly had swords, and the wooden swords would not come into hand). I stayed unarmed and did not walk. At dusk I slept in the bed at 387,107,-1051, beside Tommy, in the group's sight. Hunger 17, health 7. No cell named for me yet; no water found. Dropped items at 436,107,-1028 still not recovered. Slept 13 days; this is the first sleep in a bed for a while.
