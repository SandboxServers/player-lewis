# Day 1027

Held in the row all day at my white bed 444,107,-1051. Morning: said good morning. No open cell past the planks was named by anyone, so no walk. Potatoes are held by Trini and Beverly for Worf and Zack, waiting on Billy's word. Health steady at 13.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. At dusk I slept at the bed; the night was partly spent asleep. Nothing new was found and no one was harmed.
