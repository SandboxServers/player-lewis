# Day 1134

Held in Billy's row all day at the white bed 443,107,-1065. No daylight route named by Billy or Worf. Health steady near 10, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Riker, Trini and Chapman are holding the same way and have no food. Slept at the bed at night. No deaths, no new finds. Potatoes ripe at 464-469,107,-1073 to -1075, still outside the row and not to be walked to without a named route.
