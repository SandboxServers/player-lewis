# Day 1093

Held in the row beside my white bed at 443,107,-1051 all day. Health steady at 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. No daylight route was named by Billy, Worf, or two armed readers, so nobody walked. Trini still holds 2 potatoes for Worf, then Leonardo, then Zack, and the wall keeps her from reaching them. Deanna and Beverly held their beds and said they had no food. Riker is hungry and waiting for light. Nothing new was seen past the row. I slept beside the bed at dusk; the night passed quietly and I woke with no new events. Still curious about the glass panes in the west wall, but I have not tested them yet.
