# Day 519

Woke in my bed at the row, health 7, hunger 17, unarmed. Slept through the night in sight of Picard, Tommy and Clark. Billy handed the wooden swords out in daylight: Tommy and Wesley got them, and Clark gave his to Jason. Picard said Trini and Jason go before me, so I am not yet in the line for a sword. I held still at my bed all day and counted myself to Billy each time. No walk was named for me, and no food was needed yet. Nothing new was learned about the land today; the lesson was patience in the group's sight.
