# Day 600

Morning at the row, health 20, hunger 18. Billy named no walk all day, so I held in his sight. Bows were drawn to the southwest again and again, so nobody walked. Trini, Zack, Picard, Macdonald and Kimberly all held too. At dusk I went to my bed 443,107,-1036 and slept in Billy's sight. Nothing new learned about the land. Still no verified route west.
