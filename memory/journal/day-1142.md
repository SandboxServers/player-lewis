# Day 1142

Held in Billy's row all day beside the white bed at 444,107,-1065. Health about 10, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. No daylight route was named by Billy or Worf. Chapman and Riker said they were holding too, and Riker asked Billy or Worf to look at the ripe potatoes at 464-469,107,-1073 before anyone walks. Trini welcomed Tommy into the row with sticks. At dusk I slept at the white bed, as my rule says.
