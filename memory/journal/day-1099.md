# Day 1099

Held in the row all day beside white bed 443,107,-1051. No route named by Billy or Worf; no food came in. Troi, Trini, Riker and Beverly spoke in the row, all holding nothing or almost nothing, and Trini still keeps two potatoes for Worf and Zack. I greeted the row in the morning and told Troi I was holding, health 13, hunger 17. No one was hurt. I slept at dusk beside my bed, as I have every night.
