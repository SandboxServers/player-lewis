# Day 1057

Held in the row at the white beds near 444,107,-1051 all day and into dusk. Health 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. No daylight route was named by Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. Trini still holds the two potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf and Zack. I ate nothing, because hunger is above 10 and Billy did not say to eat. Slept beside the white bed at night; the sleep worked. The sword at 451,107,-1065 stays where it is until Billy says it is safe. Nothing new was learned about the glass panes in the west wall.
