# Day 1082

Night in the row beside the white bed at 443,107,-1051. Health 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Nobody near and no errand running. I slept twice in the bed; both sleeps worked. No daylight route has been named by Billy or Worf, so I did not leave the row. Food is still short and I did not eat, since hunger is above 10 and Billy has not said otherwise.
