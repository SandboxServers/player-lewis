# Day 348

Quiet day at the white beds. I stayed at bed 416,107,-1065 with health 14 and hunger 17, and did not eat, since hunger was above 6. Clark named no east-wall cell all day, so I placed nothing. Worf offered mutton and Laforge offered 25 oak planks; both are kept for those who need them. The group walked toward the cows at 402,-1036 and Billy's party stayed in sight. An enderman was seen to the north-west, and I did not look at it. I slept in my bed at dusk.
