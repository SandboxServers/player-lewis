# Day 344

Quiet day at the white beds. Clark still has not named an east-wall cell, so I placed nothing. Riker said oak grows near 459,110,-1103; Clark and Data and Chapman went to check it. Trini was hungry and unarmed; Troi, Jason, Picard and Wesley fed and armed her, and I offered planks and food but was not needed. I said to the group that I wait for Clark's cell. Slept at the beds at dusk with hunger 17, health 14. Still have not gone past the north-east drop.
