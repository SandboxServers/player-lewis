# Day 344

Waited at the white beds all day for Clark to name his east-wall cell. He has not, so I placed nothing. Clark checked the oak area and found only jungle logs, at 460,108-112,-1120 and 426,106-113,-1124. Riker named oak trees near 459,110,-1103, and Laforge went to look. I told Laforge I did not know of any oak nearby. Health 14, hunger 17 all day, and nothing hurt me. Slept at the white bed 416,107,-1065, in sight of the group.
