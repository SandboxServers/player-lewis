# Day 811

Woke at the bed beside the row, health and hunger 20. No walk was named by Billy all day, so I held still and greeted the row each morning. Troi and Kimberly were close to hunger 10 and will call Billy then. I kept my potato for Chapman or hunger 10, and the rotten flesh for Riker, who was not in sight. At dusk, with thunder, I slept in my bed at 443,107,-1051 beside the row. Nothing new was learned; goals stand.
