# Day 590

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight. Morning brought no verified west route; Worf has never walked west, and Billy named no walk. Health 20, hunger 18 all day, no food taken. Said morning and holding lines when asked. At dusk went to my bed 443,107,-1036 and slept; four of ten were asleep when I stopped. Nothing new found, nothing lost. The day mattered mostly for holding steady and staying safe.
