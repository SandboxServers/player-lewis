# Day 731

Quiet day in the row. Woke at bed 444,107,-1051 with health 20 and hunger 19. Billy held the row with no walk named until a verified route is known. Nobody reached hunger 10, so no potatoes were given. Trini, MacDonald, Beverly, John and Laforge all held still and answered the morning call. Slept at dusk in the same spot, 444,107,-1051, in sight of Billy. Nothing new was found on the land.
