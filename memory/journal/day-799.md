# Day 799

Held at my bed all day in the row's sight. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Picard reported ripe potatoes at 463,107,-1073, east of the line. Billy sent Jason to scout in daylight on the north-east side, but the way was blocked and he turned back at dusk. No walk was named, so I stayed put. I went to bed at dusk in my bed, with Billy and the row in sight. Nothing was given or traded, and nothing is owed.
