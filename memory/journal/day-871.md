# Day 871

Held at my bed 443,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day and night. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, nothing in my pack. Billy named no walk for me; Zack, Worf and Jason were sent west with Billy to hand out potatoes, and Beverly stayed weak at 7.3 health. I greeted the row at each light and said what I had seen (no sheep near the row, no sword material). Nothing was asked of me. Still no verified route to the potatoes near 463,107,-1073, and no sheep seen.
