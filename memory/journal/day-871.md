# Day 871

Quiet day at the bed in the row, in Billy's sight. Health and hunger stayed full. No walk was named for me, so I held still, greeted the row, and went to sleep at dusk in my bed at 443,107,-1051. Nothing new was learned today beyond the row's state: Beverly was weak and being fed potatoes, and the potatoes to the north-east are still behind a blocked route. I am still unarmed and my sword lies at 451,107,-1065.
