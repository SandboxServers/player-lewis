# Day 384

Held at the bed row all day in Billy's sight, around 429-430,107,-1035. Health and hunger stayed at 20, and I carried nothing. Billy never called the cow walk, because he kept waiting for four of us to stand together and his own health stayed low (about 10.9). Much of the day went to counting people, and I was counted as one of them. I went to sleep at the white bed at 429,107,-1036 near Billy, Tommy and Wesley, with the group in sight. The sleep returned a digest with no harm. Water is still not found, and my things at 436,107,-1028 are still not recovered.
