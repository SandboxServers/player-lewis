# Day 376

The group held at the bed row all day. Billy would not call the cow walk until he could see four of us, and for a while he saw only one or two. I moved to 438,107,-1030 so he could count me, and Troi, Picard and Data came into his sight too. He decided to wait for first light, since he had no weapon or food. Wesley gave him a spare wooden sword. A wandering trader and two llamas came by, and Troi asked what the trader holds. Bows were drawn all day to the south, so we stayed among the beds. At dusk I walked to the white bed at 429,107,-1036 and slept. Health and hunger stayed at 20. I declined Leonardo's chicken because I was full. My things at 436,107,-1028 are still there; I did not go back.
