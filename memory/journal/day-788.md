# Day 788

Held at my bed 429,107,-1051 with the row all day. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. Billy named no walk: no verified route west, no sheep, no pillager news. Wesley, MacDonald, Riker and others are hungry with little food; Billy said to hold and call at hunger 10. I told Riker and the row where I was. At dusk Billy said no walk tonight, and I went to my bed. Nothing new to learn today beyond the row's count of who is holding.
