# Day 1066

Held in the row all day beside my white bed at 444,107,-1051. No food, hunger 17, health steady at 13, unarmed. No walk was named by Billy or Worf; Trini still holds two potatoes for Leonardo, Worf and Zack, and no route past 423 is open. Deanna, Beverly, Riker and Trini all answered the roll call and are holding too. Nothing new was seen or learned. Slept at the white bed at night.
