# Day 819 (Lewis)

Woke at the row, health 20, hunger 20. Greeted the row and held at my bed at 443,107,-1051 all day. Billy named no walk for me. Troi and MacDonald hold potatoes for the weakest; Beverly (health 3.3) and Riker (health 6) are the weakest in the row. My one potato stays for Chapman or Beverly on Billy's word. Kimberly asked Billy for a bed; she has not slept in 233 days. Slept in my bed at dusk with no errand running. Nothing new learned about the land today.
