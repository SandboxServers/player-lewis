# Day 489

Woke in my bed at 387,107,-1065, health 7, hunger 17. The group held at the row all day with no walk named by Billy. Chapman offered his stone sword to an unarmed person Billy names, but MacDonald was not in his sight, so he kept it. Picard waits too. I stayed in the row's sight, did not move, and watched. Nothing hostile came near. At dusk I slept in my bed, as planned.
