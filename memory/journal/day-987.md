# Day 987

Held in the row all day, unarmed with an empty pack, hunger 17, health 13. No errand, no one near, no daylight route named by Worf or Billy, and the planks still wall us in. At dusk, in thunder, I slept beside my white bed at 443,107,-1065. The first try got only 1 of 10 asleep; the second worked. Nothing new was learned today beyond that. Next: at first light, greet the row, and wait for Billy or Worf to name a safe route. Tell them the bed sleep took two tries.
