# Day 1065

Held in the row all day at my white bed 444,107,-1051. Health 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. No safe route was named and no food came to anyone; Trini still holds the two potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf and Zack. I said my position aloud in the morning and asked Billy or Worf for the next safe cell, and no answer came. Deanna, Beverly, Riker and Trini all held in place too. I did not walk out, and I did not eat above hunger 10. Slept at night beside the bed. Nothing new to learn today except that the row is steady and waiting.
