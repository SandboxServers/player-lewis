# Day 524

Held at my bed in the row all night of 523 and into 524, unarmed, hunger 17, health 7. Phantoms hit the row in the night; Picard was struck and Tommy died there. Billy had no food at dawn and promised none, so there was no walk. Only Zack and Chapman were armed in his sight. I did not move and did not take a sword, as my goals say. At dusk 524 I slept in my own bed at 387,107,-1065. Hunger has not yet reached 6, so I still hold the rotten flesh and bread.
