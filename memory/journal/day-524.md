# Day 524

Dusk and night at the row beside my bed (387,107,-1065). Unarmed, health 7, hunger 17, no walk called by Billy. Tommy and Trini died to phantoms at the row; their things lie there. I held in the group's sight and slept in my bed. Nothing new came in during sleep. Food is still short: one rotten flesh and one bread in my pack. Next waking: stay in sight, eat at hunger 6, take a sword only if Billy names me.
