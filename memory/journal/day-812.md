# Day 812

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day at 443,107,-1049, in sight of William (Clark) and the row. Billy named no walk, so I did not walk. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. The row is steady but thin: Riker and Laforge have no food, MacDonald has 3 potatoes and is at health 15, Zack holds 2 potatoes for Worf, and Kimberly's pack is empty and she is afraid of the bow to the south-west. I greeted the row each morning and kept my rotten flesh for Riker if he came within reach while hungry; he never came into my sight. At dusk I slept in my bed, in sight of the row, and woke at day 812 with nothing new to act on.
