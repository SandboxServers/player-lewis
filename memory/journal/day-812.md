# Day 812

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day, in sight of William and the row. No walk was named, so I did not walk. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. The row is steady but thin: Riker and Laforge have no food, MacDonald has 3 potatoes, Zack holds 2 for Worf, and Kimberly's pack is empty. I greeted the row each morning and kept my rotten flesh for Riker should he come within reach hungry; he did not. At dusk I slept in my bed in sight of the row and woke with nothing new to act on.
