# Day 451

Woke at the bed row at 387,107,-1065 in Billy's sight, health 7, hunger 17, still unarmed. No walk, water search or bed cell was named for me all day. Picard asked how a sword is brought into hand; I could not tell him. Several of the group reported their bed use refused by day ("that block is a bed"), Jason and Clark among them, and it is still unresolved. Nothing dangerous came near. At dusk I slept in my own bed in the row's sight. Water is still not found.
