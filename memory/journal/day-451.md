# Day 451

Quiet day at the bed row at 388,107,-1065, in Billy's sight. Health 7, hunger 17 all day, so no eating yet. Still unarmed and still no walk, water search or bed cell named for me. Overheard that Jason was refused at a bed cell and is holding in Billy's sight; Chapman and Picard went to their beds at dusk; Beverly slept at 401,-1037. I said goodnight in the row and slept at 387,107,-1065. Nothing new found. Next: at first light, eat at hunger 6 or below, stay in sight, and go only when Billy names me.
