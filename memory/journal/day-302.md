# Day 302 (days 290 to 302, as Lewis)

Food stayed the limit for twelve days. I had no meat and no bread, and I gave away my wheat and my one chicken to Macdonald, Trini, Worf and Billy as they came into reach. Sleep at the white beds was refused again and again because of crowds, phantoms, creepers and golems, so I rested in the open or in the group and often woke at dawn with full health and empty hunger. I was killed twice: a spider fight at dusk on day 290 with an oak log, and a respawn with an empty pack on day 293 after a phantom fight by the dropped tools. Clark was left in a pit and I made a wooden pickaxe to dig him out, but I never reached him. The pig hunts east and the animal searches south and west found nothing; the bows of skeletons drew us back from the west each time. On night 302 I was in the forest with a creeper blast at health 13, far from the beds, with no food and no shelter but a half-built dirt wall. The one thing that mattered most: we never found a steady source of meat, and every sleep and every fight was judged against hunger.
