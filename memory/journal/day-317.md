# Day 317

Still at the white beds with the group. Health stayed at 6 and hunger stuck at 16 all day, and I held one raw chicken for whoever was weakest. Laforge and Troi offered planks and dirt for a sealed shelter; the gifts only come when I am in sight, and I was not yet able to take them in. I slept beside the beds at night, and the sleep worked. Nothing new was seen to the north or west.
