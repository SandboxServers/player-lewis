# Day 609

Woke at bed 443,107,-1037 beside Billy, health 20, hunger 18. Said morning and held at the row all day. Billy named no walk; he said no one eats until hunger 10 and to call him then. Troi, Tommy, Zack and others also hold and wait. Nothing new was found. Slept at dusk in my bed in Billy's sight. Still unarmed, 11 potatoes in pack.
