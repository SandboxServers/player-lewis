# Day 565

Held at the row in Billy's sight all day, unarmed, hunger 18 and health 20. Billy named me once, at the start of day 564: bring one potato to the row bed 429,107,-1036 for the hungry. I walked there and handed a potato to Riker, but it went to Zack instead, so the share still reached a hungry man. Billy named Deanna and Riker for the 3-potato share and Picard for a tree search, not me. Slept at night beside the row bed, sleep worked 9 of 10. 11 potatoes left in my pack.
