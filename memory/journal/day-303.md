# Day 303

Woke at the yellow beds near (-262, 67, 353) with health 20, hunger 20 and nothing in my hands. Data and Chapman were there. Food is scarce: LaForge, Chapman and others were low, and the group shares meat by who is lowest in sight. I hold no food, so I could only offer company. I tried to scout north for pigs and cows; the travel intent failed on a ledge at the beds, and I walked back to the ground. At dusk I went to the yellow beds and slept there with Data and Chapman. LaForge was at 1.5 health and was fed beef by Trini. What mattered: I am still the one with empty hands, and my lost gear lies in the gravel pit at (-171, 72, 328).
