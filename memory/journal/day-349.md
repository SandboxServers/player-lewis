# Day 349

Dusk at the white bed 416,107,-1065 with the group. Health 14, hunger 17. I slept in the bed beside the group and woke at morning. Clark has still named no east-wall cell, so I placed nothing on guesses. Worf, Trini, Clark and MacDonald are the ones who most need food; I hold beef and chicken for the weakest. Nothing new was found today. Phantoms were out last night and no one reached the bed row with harm to me.
