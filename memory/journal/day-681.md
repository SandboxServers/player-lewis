# Day 681

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day, sight kept, no walk named. Health 20, hunger 19 the whole day; nobody in the row needed food yet, and Billy said no feeding until hunger 10. Billy asked again whether anyone had seen a verified route west or a sheep. I answered that I had seen neither, only the row. Kimberly, Trini, Troi and Zack all say the same. Nothing new was learned about the land. Slept at my bed at night in Billy's sight; days since sleep reset to 0.

People: Billy (trust high, leads the row, names every walk); Kimberly and Trini (steady, honest, holding near); Troi/Deanna (honest, hunger 16 and watching it); Zack (has 3 potatoes and offers food); Picard and Chapman hold steady. Nothing asked of me beyond holding in sight.

Next: keep holding until Billy names a walk with escorts in sight; eat a potato at hunger 10 or on Billy's word.
