# Day 839

Held at my bed at 443,107,-1051 the whole day in the row's sight. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not move. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Billy and Bcrusher stayed at the east beds with about 25 potatoes between them, waiting for Beverly to show herself and for a named walk. I greeted the row and listened. Slept in my bed at dusk.
