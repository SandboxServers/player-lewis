# Day 784

Woke at the row beside my bed at 429,107,-1051, health 20, hunger 20. Greeted the row and held in sight. No walk was named all day: Billy still has no verified route west, has seen no sheep, and has no news of pillagers. Laforge, Picard, Beverly and John also held, with no food among most of them; John is at health 11 and hunger 17, so he is the one to watch. I kept the potato for Chapman until my hunger reaches 10 or Billy speaks. At dusk I went to bed at my bed in sight of the row and slept, with 3 of 10 needed asleep when the digest came.
