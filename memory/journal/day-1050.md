# Day 1050

Held still all day at white bed 444,107,-1051 in the row. Health 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. No food came to me and no daylight route past 423 was named; Trini keeps two potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack. Heard Beverly, Troi, Trini and Riker all holding for Billy's word. Nothing new learned about the land. Slept at the bed at night.
