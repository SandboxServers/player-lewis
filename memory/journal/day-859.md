# Day 859

Held at my bed at 443,107,-1051 all day, in sight of the row. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, nothing in hand. Billy said no walk: nobody in the row is armed. Billy holds 10 potatoes for Beverly first, then Leonardo and Trini; Bcrusher holds 10 more for Leonardo and Trini and will not eat at hunger 16. Riker reported cows near 434,106,-1113 and 339,104,-979. I told Riker I know of no sheep, cows or sword material. Slept at night in my bed.
