# Day 342

Woke at the white beds, health 14, hunger 17, nobody in sight at first. Said good morning and held the beef and chicken for the weakest. Melon is with Clark and Beverly, and Trini (health 7) is holding her melon until she reaches 6. Clark still names no east-wall cell, so I placed nothing. Spent the day at the bed row and slept at 416,107,-1065 at dusk. Nothing new was found; the day was mostly waiting, and I should go and look at something new soon.
