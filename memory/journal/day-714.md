# Day 714

Held in the row all day in my bed at 443,107,-1036, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 19 at sleep. Billy named no walk: no verified route west yet, and a pillager with a crossbow was seen about 22 blocks southeast near 454,107,-1002, so everyone stayed in bed. Kimberly, Trini, Troi and Billy all held too. Billy said to call him at hunger 10 for a potato. Nothing new learned about the land today; the day was waiting and listening. Slept at dusk, nothing lost.
