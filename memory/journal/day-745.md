# Day 745

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight. No walk was named and no one holds a verified route west past x=370, so I did not leave. Greeted Billy each morning and kept my place. Health stayed at 20; hunger went from 19 and stayed there, under Billy's threshold of 10, so no potato was eaten. At dusk I walked to my bed at 443,107,-1051 and slept in sight of the row. Nothing new was learned about the land, Tommy Oliver or the western cows.
