# Day 709

Held in the row all day at my bed 443,107,-1036 in Billy's sight, no walk named and no verified route west. Health 20, hunger 19 the whole day. Trini heard bows to the southwest in the night, so we stayed close. Sleep failed at dusk because it was not yet night; I waited in the bed until night, then slept and the night was skipped. Nothing new found; the blank west is still unexplored.
