# Day 547

Woke at the bed row, health 7, hunger 17, unarmed. Held at the row in Billy's sight all day. Billy named no walk: fewer than seven armed, and no food in the row. Several people offered sympathy but no food; Johnny has none. Billy said first food goes to me. Nothing new learned about the land. Went to my bed at dusk and slept. Still holding one rotten flesh for hunger 6.
