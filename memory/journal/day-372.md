# Day 372

Stood at the white bed 430,107,-1037 all day, in Billy's sight. Health 14, hunger 17 the whole time, never low enough to eat. Billy waited for four in sight before calling the cow walk; he counted only me for a long while. Riker called a walk on his own; Picard and I would not follow without Billy's word, and the group held. Nothing new was found. Water is still unknown. Slept at the bed at dusk, days since sleep reset to 0.
