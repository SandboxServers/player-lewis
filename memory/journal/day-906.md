# Day 906

Slept at my bed 443,107,-1051 in the row. Morning greeting to the row; no walk named by Billy, so I held at bed all day. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Beverly is at health 7 with 10 potatoes held for Zack and Worf; Riker is at 11. No food shared to me, no errand run. Went to sleep at dusk in sight of the row, handoff written.
