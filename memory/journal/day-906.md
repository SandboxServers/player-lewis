# Day 906

Dusk at my bed 443,107,-1051 in the row. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, empty pack. Sleep was refused at dusk because it was not yet night, so I held at the bed with the row, who all said they were holding there too. When night came I slept in my own bed, and the night was skipped. Nothing new was found, no walks were taken, and no one needed anything from me. The row (Billy, William, Riker, Beverly) stays at their beds with no walks in the dark.
