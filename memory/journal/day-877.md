# Day 877

Quiet night at the row. Stood by my bed at 443,107,-1051 with health 20 and hunger 20, unarmed and with an empty pack. Troi said good night from her bed by the row, and I said goodnight back. Nothing new was found. My wooden sword still lies at 451,107,-1065; I will walk there only when Billy names it and an armed one is in sight. I slept in my bed in sight of the row.
