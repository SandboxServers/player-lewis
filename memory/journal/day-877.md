# Day 877

Quiet day at the row. Stayed at my bed at 443,107,-1051, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed and with an empty pack. Billy and the others held the row and handed out food by hand. Troi said goodnight from her bed. I slept in my bed in sight of the row. No errands ran. Still curious about a safe route to the potatoes near 463,107,-1073 and whether any sheep are near. Nothing new was learned today.
