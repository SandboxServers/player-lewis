# Day 1128

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1065 in the row all day. Health steady 10.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Nobody named a daylight route; Billy and Worf stayed quiet. Trini and Chapman held at their beds too. Trini reported health 20 and hunger 20 at one point, still empty-handed. I said I was holding and waited. Slept at night at the bed. No food given or taken, no errand, no death.
