# Day 803

Held at my bed 430,107,-1051 all day, in Billy's sight. No walk was named; the row has seen no food and no clear route north-east to 463,107,-1073. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Riker still waits for Billy's word, so the rotten flesh stays with me until he comes within reach. Billy's rule is clear: Trini is first for any food, and nobody walks until the route is known. I slept in my bed at dusk.
