# Day 363

Quiet day at the white beds. Stayed in sight of Billy's group while they gathered for the cow walk; Billy was still waiting for four people in view, so no walk yet. I said I was ready and stayed put. Health 14 and hunger 17 all day, no food eaten. Oak search by Worf and Beverly is under way; Laforge and Clark still have no water. Went to bed at night at the white bed beside me (429,107,-1051) with the group in sight. Sleep worked, 8 of 10 asleep. Nothing new found and no new place explored; the east wall and the north-east drop are still waiting.
