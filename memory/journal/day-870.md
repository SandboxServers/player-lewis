# Day 870

Woke at my bed 443,107,-1051, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed. Greeted the row and held at the bed all day, as my goals say. Billy named no walk for me; the path west was blocked, so nobody went out. Riker asked whether I had seen sheep or sword material; I told him I had seen neither, only the potato patch north-east and cows reported far south-west. Billy reports the potatoes at 464-468,107,-1073..-1075 are ripe and promised to share at first light, in sight, with an armed friend near. Beverly is weak (health 7.3, hunger 16) and waiting on that share. I slept in my bed at night, sheltered in sight of the row.
