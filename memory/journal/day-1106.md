# Day 1106

Held in the row at my white bed 444,107,-1051 all day, health 13, hunger 17, no food, pack empty. Heard Trini and Riker at 423 still see only planks past it, so no route was named and I broke nothing. Troi, Beverly, Trini and Riker all said they are waiting on Billy's routing; no one has food to spare for me yet. I greeted Troi at light and kept still. Slept at dusk beside the white bed, as before.
