# Day 648

Held in Billy's row all day. No verified west route, so Billy named no walk; I held at my bed with hunger at 19 and health 20 the whole time. Said morning, told Billy I had seen no route or Wesley. Dusk came and I slept in my bed at 443,107,-1036 in Billy's sight. Nothing new was learned today, and nothing was eaten. Next: eat at hunger 10 or on Billy's word.
