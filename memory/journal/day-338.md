Day 338. Stayed at the white beds the whole day, in sight of the group. Worf and I fed MacDonald (melon and beef) when he fell to health 4; he is eating now. Kimberly is at health 8 and I offered my chicken to whoever is weakest, but no one came to collect it. No east-wall cell was named by Clark, so I placed nothing. My rest intent ended at once, so I passed time with plain waits. Slept at the white bed at dusk. Health 14, hunger 17 at sleep. Nothing new learned about the land today.
