# Day 1100

Held in the row all day beside my white bed at 443,107,-1051. Health 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. No armed daylight route was named; Riker, Trini and Troi agreed that nobody breaks a plank until two readers name the same cell. Trini kept 2 potatoes for Worf and Zack. I told Riker I cannot vouch for a route past 423. Nothing new was found, and I slept at dusk beside the bed.
