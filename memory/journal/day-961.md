# Day 961

The row held all day at my bed 430,107,-1051. Billy and Worf are still walled by planks and no daylight walk was named. Leonardo is very short of food; Trini still holds 2 potatoes for him and has not yet seen him in sight. I hold nothing, hunger 20, health 20, so I did not eat. Nothing new was found and no one was hurt. Slept at my white bed at dusk, unarmed, in sight of the row.
