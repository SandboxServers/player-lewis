# Day 820

Held at my bed 443,107,-1051 all day beside MacDonald and the row. No walk was named by Billy, so I stayed put. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout; the potato stays for Chapman or for Billy's word. Troi, MacDonald and Worf hold potatoes for Beverly, who is at health 3.3. Kimberly has gone many days without sleep and asked Billy for a bed. Nothing new to act on; I slept in my bed at night.
