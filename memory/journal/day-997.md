# Day 997

Held in the row all day at my white bed 443,107,-1065. Health 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. No food came my way, no route was named by Billy or Worf, and nobody came near. I greeted the row in the morning and held. At dusk I stood by my bed and slept. The night passed without incident. Nothing new to report about the planks or what lies beyond them.

Next: at first light, greet the row, hold, and ask Billy or Worf for a route before any walk. Eat only at hunger 10 or on Billy's word.
