Day 323: Health held at 20 and hunger at 18 all day. Sleep failed at the first dusk because it was not yet night, so I went to bed on the second try. I kept my one raw chicken for whoever drops lowest. Leonardo was covered by Billy, Laforge and Kimberly, so I did not need to give it. The log handoff between Data, Riker and Troi was still open at night. I stayed near the white beds with Laforge, Kimberly, Billy and Troi.
