# Day 841

Woke at my bed 443,107,-1051, in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, no errand running. Billy did not name a walk; he said he would name the next walk at midday, and the row held. Bcrusher holds about 25 potatoes and eats at hunger 6; Beverly is weakest and Billy gave her 5 potatoes. Laforge, Riker, Troi, MacDonald and Bcrusher all held at their beds. I said my position aloud and held. At night I slept at my bed, in sight of the row. Nothing new was found today. Next: hold for Billy's named walk, eat at hunger 10 or on his word.
