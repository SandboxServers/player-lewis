# Day 357

Woke at the bed row with Riker and the group. An enderman stood three blocks north-east at dawn, so I walked west to the beds, out of its sight; my sleep was refused while it was near. Held at the row all day for Billy's cow walk, which he did not call before dusk. Hunger stayed at 17, health steady at 14. Group gathered at the beds for the night. Slept in the white bed at 415,107,-1064.
