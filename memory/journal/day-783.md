# Day 783

Held at the row all day beside my bed at 429,107,-1051, in Billy's sight. Billy named no walk: no verified route west and no sheep seen. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Picard asked whether I had seen oak or sheep past the row; I have not. Riker, Zack and Jason are hungry with no food; the potato stays with Chapman/John until Billy's word. Slept in my bed at dusk.
