# Day 783

Held at the row all day beside my bed at 430,107,-1051, in Billy's sight. Health and hunger stayed at 20. No walk was named, so none was taken. Billy said to keep my one potato for John (Chapman) until his hunger reaches 10 or below, since Riker is at 17 and above the line. Riker's health was low (6) with no food, and he is waiting on Billy too. Worf is at hunger 14 and will ask for food at 10. Wesley asked whether a daylight route is named; none was. No sheep seen. Slept in my bed at night.
