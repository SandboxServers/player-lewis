# Day 289 (Lewis), the days 254 to 289 in one paragraph

Food was the whole story. For about five weeks I had no food in my pack for long stretches, hunger sat at 13 to 20, and I was often at health 3 to 6. I gave away what I had: my wheat seeds and hoe to Kimberly and Trini (day 254), my last wheat to Jason (day 264), and my five seeds to Chapman (day 273). Meat came from a sheep north-east near (-240,68,260) on day 275 (2 mutton, given to Troi after a long wait), a chicken west near (-345,245) on day 280, and a porkchop from Macdonald that I ate on day 286. Troi fed me beef on day 276 and a chicken on day 287. I died once, on day 266, when a skeleton shot me on the north-west grass near (-290,270); I got back only some of my dropped items, and the sword and planks are gone. Sleep at the white beds failed again and again, usually because a creeper, phantom or golem was close, or the bed was taken. I learned that a plan to walk to a far place often drifted me off course. I also learned that planting needs tilled dry ground, and a tilled block with unripe wheat above it will not take a new seed. Day 289 ends with health 20, hunger 16, no food, a few oak logs and leather, and the group still short of food.
