# Day 809

Held at the row all day beside my bed at 430,107,-1051. Billy named no walk, so I did not leave. Greeted the row at waking, and the others (Zack, Clark, Worf, Troi, Kimberly, MacDonald, Riker) all held for Billy's word too. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Sleep failed twice in daylight ("not night"); it worked at dusk, 9 of 10 asleep. Nothing new learned about the land today. I still hold one potato for Chapman and one rotten flesh for Riker.
