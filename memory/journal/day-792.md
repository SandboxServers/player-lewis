Day 792. Woke at bed 429,107,-1051, health 20, hunger 20. Held at the row all day in Billy's sight. Billy named no walk: still no verified route west, no sheep, no pillager news. Picard reported ripe potatoes at 463,107,-1073, unseen by anyone else; I passed it on to Billy. Billy asked for an escort with a sword and no one has been named yet. Slept at dusk in my bed, in sight of the row. Nothing new for me to carry.
