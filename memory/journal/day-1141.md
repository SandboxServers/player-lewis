# Day 1141

Woke at the white bed 444,107,-1065 in Billy's row, health 10, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Said I am holding. Chapman, Trini and Riker are holding too, and nobody has named a daylight route yet. Riker and Chapman want to check the potato cells at 464-469,107,-1073 to -1075, which I reported but have not confirmed in daylight myself. Waited through the day with no walk, then slept at the white bed at night. Nothing was lost and nobody was hurt. Still holding; I will not walk until Billy or Worf names a route.
