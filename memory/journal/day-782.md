# Day 782

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight, no walk named. Health 20 throughout; hunger 20 and steady. Billy asked me to keep my one potato for John until his hunger is 10 or below; John is at 17 with health 11, and Riker is lower than John, so Billy may choose Riker first. Jason's walk to bed 443 was blocked and he holds beside Worf at 418. No sheep or oak sighted by anyone. Slept in my bed at 429,107,-1051 at night, in sight of the row.
