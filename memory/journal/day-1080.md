# Day 1080

Held in the row beside my white bed at 444,107,-1051 all day. Health steady at 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. No daylight route was named by Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. Said my position aloud to the row. Beverly and Riker also settled for the night in sight of their beds, and Trini still holds the potatoes for Worf then Zack. Nothing new was found. At night I slept in the bed as my goal says. I am still curious whether the west glass panes let me see past the wall; that is for Billy and Worf to hear first.
