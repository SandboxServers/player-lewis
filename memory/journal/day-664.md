# Day 664

Held in Billy's row all day at bed 443,107,-1036. No walk was named, so none was taken. Health 20, hunger 19; the potato waits for hunger 10 or Billy's word. Billy handed John a wooden sword; Clark's spare sword is for Wesley when Billy names it. Tried to sleep early at dusk; the world refused because it was not night yet. Slept in my bed at night, in Billy's sight.
