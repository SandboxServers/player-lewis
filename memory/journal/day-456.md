# Day 456

Waited the whole day at the bed row, 388,107,-1065, in sight of Jason, Tommy, Trini and Beverly. Health stayed at 7 and hunger at 17, so I kept the rotten flesh and bread for hunger 6. Nobody named a walk, a water search or a sword step for me. Billy holds the stone sword but cannot get it into hand. I greeted Jason and asked Billy about the sword step twice, with no answer yet. At dusk I went to bed at 387,107,-1065 and slept in sight of the row. Still unarmed, water not found, and the dropped items at 436,107,-1028 are not recovered.
