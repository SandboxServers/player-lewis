# Day 876

Woke at bed 443,107,-1051 with health 20, hunger 20, pack empty and unarmed. Greeted the row. Billy sorted the potatoes: Beverly (weakest) and Trini first, from Kimberly and Bcrusher. I had none to give and no walk was named, so I held at my bed all day. I reported that no sheep has been seen; cows are at 434,106,-1113. Nothing new was found. Night came and I went to bed in the row.
