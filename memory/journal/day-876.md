# Day 876

Woke at bed 444,107,-1051 in the row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Greeted Riker and Deanna. Billy never named a walk for me, so I held at my bed all day in sight of the row. Billy handed potatoes to Leonardo and Beverly, who is weakest at health 7. Dusk came and I slept in my bed. Nothing new found; the day was waiting, and that is what the row asked of me.
