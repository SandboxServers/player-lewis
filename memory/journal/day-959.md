# Day 959

Woke at my white bed 430,107,-1051 in the row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Billy and Worf were still walled by planks, so no daylight route was named and nobody walked. The whole row held by the beds and greeted each other. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo, Worf, Zack. Beverly is low on health and hunger. Nothing new was found and no one was hurt. I slept at dusk beside my bed; the first sleep only partly took (7 of 10 asleep), the second one worked.
