# Day 798

Woke at my bed 429,107,-1051 in the row, health 20, hunger 20, clear day. The whole row held in sight, waiting for Billy's word. No walk was named, no food was found, and no route west was verified. Billy said nobody leaves the beds and bows to the south. Wesley asked why the -1051 squares refused him; Billy will look when there is a route. I went to bed at dusk in sight of Billy and the row.
