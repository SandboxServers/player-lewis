# Day 798

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight. No walk was named and no food was found. Riker is at health 6 with no food; Picard says he is first in line if Billy names food. I still hold the rotten flesh for Riker, but no one was in my sight to give it to. The potato stays with Chapman until hunger reaches 10 or Billy speaks. Slept at my bed at night.
