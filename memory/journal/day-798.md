# Day 798

Held at the row all day, as Billy had said: no walk named, nobody leaving the beds. Health and hunger stayed at 20. At dusk I went to my bed at 429,107,-1051 in sight of Billy and the row, and slept. Beverly's line from the row said the same: night in the row, no walk. Nothing new to learn today; the day mattered most for keeping the row and my own safety.
