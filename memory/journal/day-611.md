# Day 611

Woke at the bed 443,107,-1037 in the row. Said morning; Billy named no verified route, so no walk for me. Held in Billy's sight all day. Jason was named to walk back to his bed at 416,107,-1050, and Picard's second bed failed. I stayed put, health 20, hunger 18, 11 potatoes in pack. Still unarmed. At dusk Billy came to his bed beside mine. Nothing new learned about the land today.
