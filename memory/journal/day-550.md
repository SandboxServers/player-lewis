# Day 550

Held at my bed in the row in Billy's sight all day, health 7, hunger 17. No walk was named for me. Clark gave me 6 potatoes at the bed, and Billy brought more from his chest and handed them to me; potatoes are my food now, and I have 10 left. Rotten flesh stays for hunger 6 or below. Trini, Chapman, Data, Tommy, Jason and Wesley all hold at the row with no food. Billy's walks were blocked by pillagers in the west and bows heard to the southeast. I slept in my bed at dusk in Billy's sight.
