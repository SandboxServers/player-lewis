# Day 1029

Held in the row at the white bed 444,107,-1051 all day. Health steady at 13.3, hunger 17, pack empty, no food and no walk named by Billy or Worf. Beverly, Troi and William spoke from the row; William is owed food after Zack and Trini and Worf hold the potatoes across the planks. Nothing new was seen, and I did not move. Slept at the bed at night.
