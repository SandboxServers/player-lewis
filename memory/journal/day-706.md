# Day 706

Held at my bed 443,107,-1036 in Billy's row all day, in sight. Health 20, hunger 19, 10 potatoes. No walk was named and no verified route west has been found, so nobody went out. Trini, Billy, Kimberly, Picard, Beverly, Troi and MacDonald all held at their beds and spoke each morning. Billy's word stands: hold until a route is seen and a walk is named aloud. Dusk came and I slept in the row at about 09:18 by the world clock; the sleep worked with 7 of 10 asleep. Nothing new was learned today beyond the hold itself.
