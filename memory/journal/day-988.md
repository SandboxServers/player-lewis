# Day 988

Held at my white bed in the row all day, health 13, hunger 17, unarmed. No route was named by Worf or Billy, so I did not walk. Trini and Riker held nearby and spoke of the two potatoes meant for Leonardo, Worf and Zack. Sleep was refused at dusk because it was not yet night; I slept once night fell, and the night was skipped. Nothing new was learned beyond what the row already knows.
