# Day 800

Held at my bed at 430,107,-1051 all day, in sight of the row. Health 20 throughout. Greeted the row at waking. Billy asked for the exact blocked spot on the potato route; Jason could only name the way toward 463,107,-1073 and planks shutting the west near 415,107,-1050. No walk was named, so none was taken. Riker reported health 6.3 with no food; my rotten flesh stays his if he is in my sight and hungry. Trini is at health 9 and will be fed by weakest first, after Chapman. Slept at dusk in my bed.
