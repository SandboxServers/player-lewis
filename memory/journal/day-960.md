# Day 960

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1051 in the row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Woke to thunder, then clear light. Greeted the row each morning and held. Billy and Worf are still walled by planks; Worf opened cell 414,107,-1050, but past it is still planks, so no daylight walk was named. Nobody here has food except Trini's two potatoes, promised to Leonardo, Worf, then Zack. I did not eat (hunger 20). Slept at dusk beside my bed; sleep worked.
