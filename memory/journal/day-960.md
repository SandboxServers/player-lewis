# Day 960

Held in the row all day at my white bed 430,107,-1051. Walls still whole; Billy and Worf found no open cell, so no daylight route was named and nobody walked. Health and hunger stayed at 20. No food in my pack, nothing to share. Talk in the row was of Trini's potatoes (for Leonardo, Worf, Zack) and Beverly's low health. Went to bed at dusk and slept; the sleep worked on the first try.
