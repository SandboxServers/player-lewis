# Day 1103

Night in the row, dark and clear. Nobody near, no errand, no danger. I stayed beside the white bed at 443,107,-1051, health 13, hunger 17, pack empty. The first sleep said 7 of 10 were asleep; I then slept through the night and woke to a new day. No food found, no route named, nothing new seen. Holding in the row for Billy's routing at first light.
