# Day 715

Held in the row at my bed 443,107,-1036 all day, in Billy's sight. No walk was named: Billy still has no verified route west. Health stayed at 20 and hunger fell from 20 to 19 at sleep. Billy said to call him at hunger 10. Troi, MacDonald and Trini held at their beds too. Nothing new was found, and nothing was lost. I slept in my bed at night, with no errand running.
