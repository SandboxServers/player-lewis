Day 334. Went north-east past the birch to 476,110,-1112 and found ground that drops about six blocks, in four spots, toward the north-east. Walked back to the bed row and told the group. Tried to follow Troi to her drops at 408,-1050; the follow failed because she was out of sight. Clark still names no open east-wall cell. Slept at the white bed by 415,107,-1065 with health 14 and hunger 17. Nobody was hurt.
