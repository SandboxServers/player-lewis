# Day 334

Slept at white bed 416,107,-1065 with health 14 and hunger 17. Went north-east past the birch earlier and found the ground dropping about six blocks toward the north-east at 476,110,-1112. Enderman was reported near camp at night; I stayed at the bed. Clark still has named no east-wall cell. Picard, Bcrusher, Laforge and Data each said they are holding melon or chicken for the weakest at first light.
