# Day 334 (Lewis)

Backed away from an enderman at the bed row and it left. Walked north-east to the birch ground at 463-468, 109-112, z -1102 to -1118, with jungle logs at 460,108-112,-1120 and 480,109-111,-1104, and ripe wheat at 463,107,-1087 and -1088. Nobody in the group has been past the birch yet. Came back to the white bed at 416,107,-1064 before dark and slept there. Health 14, hunger 17; food was offered by several people but kept for the weakest.
