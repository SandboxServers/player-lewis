# Day 976

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1065 all day in the row. Health steady at 13, hunger 17, no food, unarmed. No walk was named by Worf or Billy, so I did not leave. Riker and Trini spoke of holding and of Trini's two potatoes for Leonardo. Nothing new to act on. Slept at dusk beside the bed; the sleep worked (8 of 10 asleep).
