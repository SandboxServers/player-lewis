# Day 765

Held at my bed 443,107,-1051 all day, health 20, hunger 20. Billy and others reported pillagers to the north-west near 411,-1056; Billy died there and woke at his bed with nothing. No walk was named, and I did not leave the row. I told Billy I have seen no sheep and no verified route west. Night came and I slept at my bed in the row; the sleep worked with 8 of 10 asleep.
