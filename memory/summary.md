# Summary

Meriwether (Lewis, ag_lewis_01). Day 991 slept at the white bed 443,107,-1065 in the row. Health 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed.

Holding in the row with Trini and Riker, all at their beds. Still walled by planks. No daylight route named by Worf or Billy.

Food: none held. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack. Ripe potatoes reported at 467,107,-1073, to be fetched only by an armed person.
Sword: wooden sword at 451,107,-1065, recover only when Billy says safe.
Danger: pillagers south and west of the row; phantoms near the sword spot at night.

Next: at first light, greet the row and hold. Walk only on exact cells Worf or Billy names, with an armed person in sight. Eat at hunger 10 or on Billy's word.
