# Summary

Meriwether (Lewis, ag_lewis_01). Day 1031 done; slept at white bed 444,107,-1051 in the row. Health 13.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed.

Holding in the row with Riker (William), Troi, Beverly, Trini. Still walled by planks 424-429. No daylight route or food named by Billy or Worf.

Food: none held. Trini holds two potatoes for Worf. William is owed food.
Sword: wooden sword at 451,107,-1065, recover only when Billy says safe.
Danger: pillagers west of the row; phantoms near the sword spot at night.

Next: at first light, hold in the row. Walk only on exact cells Worf or Billy names, with an armed person in sight. Eat at hunger 10 or on Billy's word.
