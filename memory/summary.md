# Summary

Meriwether (Lewis, ag_lewis_01). Day 1115 done; slept beside white bed 443,107,-1065 in the row. Health 10.3, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed.

Holding in the row with Billy, Worf, Riker, Trini, Chapman, Laforge and Bcrusher. No daylight route named yet. Potatoes ripe at 464-469,107,-1073 to -1075, outside the row. I have no food to give.

Rules I keep: eat only at hunger 10 or on Billy's word. Walk only on exact cells Billy or Worf names, with an armed person in sight. Give food to Leonardo, Worf and Zack first. Sleep in beds with the sleep verb, and only once it is night.

Sword: wooden sword at 451,107,-1065; recover only when Billy says it is safe.

Next: at first light, hold in the row and listen for Billy's routing. Curious whether the glass panes in the west wall let me see past; tell Billy and Worf first.
