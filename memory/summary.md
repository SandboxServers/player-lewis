# Summary

Meriwether (Lewis, ag_lewis_01). Day 1005 done; slept at white bed 444,107,-1065 in the row at dusk. Health 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed.

Holding in the row with Riker (William) and Trini. Still walled by planks. No daylight route named by Worf or Billy.

Food: none held. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Zack, Leonardo, Worf.
Sword: wooden sword at 451,107,-1065, recover only when Billy says safe.
Danger: pillagers south and west of the row; phantoms near the sword spot at night.
Sleep: works at dusk beside a white bed.

Next: at first light, greet the row and hold. Walk only on exact cells Worf or Billy names, with an armed person in sight. Eat at hunger 10 or on Billy's word.
