Holding in the row with Billy, Worf, Riker, Beverly, Deanna, Trini, Troi and Leonardo. No daylight route named yet. Food is short: Trini holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo first, then Worf and Zack. Trini will not pass 423; two grave signs there say "shot".
