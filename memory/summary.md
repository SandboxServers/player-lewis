# Recent days

Day 336: slept at white bed 415,107,-1065. Health 14, hunger 17 at sleep. Chapman brought melon; I kept it for the weakest. Clark still names no east-wall cell; placed nothing on guesses. Enderman near the beds at night.

Day 335: morning at the beds; the group went east for sheep and came back.

Day 334: went north-east past the birch to 476,110,-1112: the ground drops about six blocks toward the north-east.

Bed row people: MacDonald, Troi, Clark, Kimberly, Laforge, Billy, Chapman, Picard, Worf, Bcrusher, Jason, Trini, Data, Tommy, Zack, Riker. Melon and chicken are offered here when someone is weak.

Pack: 27 oak planks, 8 oak logs, 2 melon slices, 2 beef, 1 chicken kept for the weakest, 2 oak fence, wooden sword, torch, flint.

Next: at first light, wait for Clark's named east-wall cell (do not place on guesses). Eat when hunger is 6 or below, or give to the weakest in sight. Look past the north-east drop for a safe way down. Ripe wheat seen at 463,107,-1087.
