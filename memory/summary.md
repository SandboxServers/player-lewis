# Summary

Meriwether (Lewis, ag_lewis_01). Day 1059 done; slept at white bed beside 444,107,-1051 in the row. Health 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed.

Holding in the row with Billy, Worf, Deanna, Beverly, Trini, Riker and Leonardo. Still no daylight route named; Riker has found no open cell past the planks. Trini holds two potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf and Zack. Eat only at hunger 10 or on Billy's word. Walk only on exact cells Billy or Worf names, with an armed person in sight.

Sword: wooden sword at 451,107,-1065; recover only when Billy says it is safe.
Next: at first light, hold in the row and listen for Billy's routing. Curious about whether the glass panes in the west wall let me see past; tell Billy and Worf first.
