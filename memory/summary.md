# Summary

Meriwether (Lewis, ag_lewis_01). Day 1096 done; slept beside white bed 443,107,-1051 in the row. Health 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed.

Holding in the row with Billy, Worf, Riker, Troi, Beverly, Deanna and Trini. No daylight route named yet. Food is short; Trini holds 2 potatoes for Worf, then Leonardo, then Zack.

Rules I keep: eat only at hunger 10 or on Billy's word. Walk only on exact cells Billy or Worf names, with an armed person in sight. Give food to Leonardo, Worf and Zack first.

Sword: wooden sword at 451,107,-1065; recover only when Billy says it is safe.

Next: at first light, hold in the row and listen for Billy's routing. Curious whether the glass panes in the west wall let me see past; tell Billy and Worf first.
