# Summary

Meriwether (Lewis, ag_lewis_01). Day 1072 done; slept at the white bed 444,107,-1051 in the row. Health 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed.

Holding in the row with Billy, Worf, Deanna, Beverly, Trini, Riker, Troi and Leonardo. No daylight route named yet. Food is short: Trini holds two potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf and Zack. Eat only at hunger 10 or on Billy's word. Walk only on exact cells Billy or Worf names, with an armed person in sight.

Sword: wooden sword at 451,107,-1065; recover only when Billy says it is safe.

Next: at first light, hold in the row and listen for Billy's routing. Curious whether the glass panes in the west wall let me see past; tell Billy and Worf first.
