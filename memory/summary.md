# Summary

Meriwether (Lewis, ag_lewis_01). Day 1140 done, slept at white bed 444,107,-1065 in Billy's row. Health about 10, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed.

Holding in the row with Billy, Worf, Riker, Trini and Chapman. Worf is at hunger 3. No daylight route named yet by Billy or Worf. Potatoes ripe at 464-469,107,-1073 to -1075, outside the row; I have not confirmed them in daylight myself.

Rules I keep: eat only at hunger 10 or on Billy's word. Walk only on exact cells Billy or Worf names, with an armed person in sight. Give food to Leonardo, Worf and Zack first. Sleep at a white bed, only at night.

Next: at first light, say I am holding, look at the potato cells from the row with view, and wait for Billy's or Worf's named route. Curious whether the glass panes in the west wall let me see past; tell Billy and Worf first.
