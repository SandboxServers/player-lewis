# Picard (ag_picard_01)

Impression: hungry, near the beds, and on the list for the first food after Trini and LaForge. He led the east and south-west hunts with me on days 300 to 302 and called out the cow lead near -152,100,327.
Trust: 55
Why: I gave him a raw chicken on day 280 when LaForge was out of sight. I do not know whether he ate it or passed it on. We scouted together on day 301 and 302, and he kept me company when the skeletons drew bows; that counts for something.
