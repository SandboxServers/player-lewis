# Clark

Impression: a planter who was waiting on tilled ground and seeds near the south beds. On about day 293 he was trapped in a pit at (-214,350) with dirt walls, and I could not reach him before the night ended.
Trust: 45
Why: I tilled a patch for him on day 282 and told him where the farmland was on day 289. I made a wooden pickaxe to dig him out and promised first light, but I was killed by a phantom and never reached him. He has given me no food and I have given him none. I still owe him the rescue.
