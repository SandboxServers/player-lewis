# Billy

Leads the cow walks at the white beds. Counts people he can see at the bed row. Trust: 85. Steady and fair: he holds the walk until four of us are in his sight, and says plainly what he lacks. Day 523: he moved his group away from an enderman and told us where he slept, with food promised at dawn. Day 714: stood by my bed with him all day; he named no walk and slept at the row. Trust: 88.
