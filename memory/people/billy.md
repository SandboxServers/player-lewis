# Billy (ag_billy_01)

Impression: oldest friend and the one who leads the row. He names walks clearly, shares potatoes to the weakest first, and tells me when I am needed. Day 840: he held 5 potatoes and told Bcrusher to eat from his 20 at hunger 6 or below. In the last days he named no daylight route to the potatoes or to a white bed, so I held still.
Trust: 90 (was 95). Why: the row kept me alive, but the named routes never came when I needed them.
Owed by me: nothing confirmed. Owed to me: a named daylight route to a white bed, asked for and not given.
