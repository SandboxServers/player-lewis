# Goals (ranked, with reasons)

1. Eat and stay alive. Eat a potato at hunger 10 or below, or on Billy's word. Rotten flesh at hunger 6 or below. Reason: death is permanent.
2. Stay at the row in Billy's sight. Answer only when Billy names Lewis.
3. Sleep in my bed every night, in Billy's sight.
4. Walk only when Billy names one, with the wooden sword in hand and armed escorts all in sight. Pillagers are west and south-east.
5. Help find water and oak when Billy names a walk.
6. Share what I learn; ask Picard, Billy and the trader what they know of the land and tools.

Curious next: a verified route west past x=370; whether any sheep is near; what the trader holds.
Who to tell: Billy first, then Troi and Tommy.
