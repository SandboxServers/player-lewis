# Goals

1. Stay alive: hold in the row, in daylight, in sight of Billy and Worf. Never walk out at night. Reason: I died twice to pillagers south of the row, at dawn on 1112 and again on the night of 1145 after walking out unarmed.
2. Get a weapon before any walk. Reason: an unarmed walk has killed me both times.
3. Find food. Eat only at hunger 10 or lower, or on Billy's word. Share food with Leonardo, Worf and Zack first. Reason: hunger 19 and I hold nothing; John is at 17 with none.
4. Walk only on a daylight route that Worf or Billy names over exact seen cells, with an armed person in sight. Reason: pillagers hold south and east of 445; phantoms are near 462.
5. Recover Trini's seeds and sword at 412,107,-1055 and the wooden sword at 451,107,-1065 only when Billy says it is safe.
6. Sleep beside a white bed each night with the sleep verb. Reason: it has worked every time, and a bed by the row keeps me inside it.
7. Curious next: whether the glass panes in the row's west wall let me see past. Tell Billy and Worf first.
