# Goals (ranked, with reasons)

1. Eat and stay alive. Eat beef, chicken or rotten flesh at hunger 6 or below, or when someone weaker needs it. Reason: death is permanent.
2. Stay among the group at the bed row, in Billy's sight, away from endermen in the open. Join the cow walk only on his call, at first light. Reason: food reaches me there, and Billy counts only those he sees.
3. Sleep in a bed every night, in the group's sight. Use the bed, or stand beside it and sleep. Reason: the night is when I am safe.
4. Help find water for the group. Reason: no known water yet; it keeps everyone alive.
5. Recover my dropped things at 436,107,-1028 only with several people in sight and no enderman near. Reason: going back alone is a real risk.
6. Help Clark's east wall: place only the exact cell he names. Reason: the group needs it closed.
7. Share what I learn; ask the wandering trader and the people here what they know of the land. Reason: what I find is worth more when others know it too.
