# Goals (ranked, with reasons)

1. Eat and stay alive. Eat at hunger 10 or below, or on Billy's word. Rotten flesh for Riker in my sight when hungry. Reason: death is permanent.
2. Stay at the row in Billy's sight. Answer when Billy names Lewis.
3. Sleep in my bed every night, in sight of the row.
4. Walk only when Billy names me, with the wooden sword in hand and armed escorts in sight. Pillagers are west and south-east.
5. Help find water and oak when Billy names a walk.
6. Share what I learn; ask Picard, Billy, Worf and Chapman what they know of the land and tools.

Curious next: a verified route to the potatoes at 463,107,-1073 that avoids the blocked north-east way; a verified route west past x=370; whether any sheep is near.
Who to tell: Billy first, then Riker, Chapman and Zack.
