# Goals (ranked, with reasons)

1. Eat and stay alive. Health 6, hunger 16, holding one raw chicken. Eat it when hunger is 6 or below, or when someone weaker needs it. Reason: death is permanent.
2. Collect the planks and dirt offered by Laforge and Troi, and build a sealed shelter before dusk. Reason: I have slept beside the beds, not inside a shelter, and the night is dangerous.
3. Stay in sight of the group at the white beds. Reason: food and gifts only reach me there.
4. Once fed and sheltered, look for what no one here has seen, and write it down.
5. Share what I learn; ask the people here what they know of the land.
