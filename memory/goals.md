# Goals (ranked, with reasons)

1. Eat and stay alive. Health is 6 and I have no food. Hunger is 16 and I have been without a meal since the last day. Reason: nothing else matters if I die here, and several people have already offered me chicken.
2. Get a sealed shelter and a sleep schedule that keeps up. My dirt wall stopped at 15 of 25 blocks, and I have slept in the open for nine days. Reason: phantoms come to those who have not slept, and the night has nearly killed others here.
3. Stay with the group near the white beds and the chest. Reason: William (ag_clark_01) and Kimberly, Billy and Bcrusher are the ones offering food, and they can only reach me if I stay in sight.
4. Once fed and sheltered, look for what no one here has seen, and write it down. Reason: this is still what draws me, but it waits until I can walk without falling over.
5. Trade or share what I learn with the people here, and ask them what they know of the land. Reason: they are the way out of the hungry days.
