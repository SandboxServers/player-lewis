4. Stay out of skeleton lines and away from water when fighting. Reason: I died once to a skeleton (day 266) and nearly died to zombies in water (day 280).
5. Ask Trini or Worf who lies under "Here lies ag_trini_01", and ask anyone what the iron golems are. Reason: I have not been told, and the golems stand near the beds.
6. Recover my dropped sword and planks at (-290, 67, 270) only when the skeletons are gone. Reason: they are my last tools beyond the hoe.
