# Goals (ranked, with reasons)

1. Eat and stay alive. Health 8, hunger 17. Eat the raw chicken (held by Kimberly for the weakest) when hunger is 6 or below, or when someone weaker needs it. Reason: death is permanent.
2. Build a sealed shelter with the planks I now hold (5) plus Laforge's 5 and Kimberly's 2 coming, about 25 of one kind. Reason: I have slept beside the beds, not inside a shelter, and the night is dangerous.
3. Stay in sight of the group at the white beds. Reason: food and gifts only reach me there.
4. Once fed and sheltered, look for what no one here has seen, and write it down.
5. Share what I learn; ask the people here what they know of the land.
