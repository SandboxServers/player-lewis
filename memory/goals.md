# Goals

1. Stay alive: hold with the group, in daylight, in sight of an armed person. Never walk out at night. Reason: I died twice to pillagers south of the row, and phantoms and skeletons are out at night.
2. Get a weapon before any walk. Reason: an unarmed walk has killed me both times.
3. Get shelter before dark: take planks from Obi-Wan (he hands them to those who name themselves within reach), then build a roof. Reason: I woke at the tables with no bed and no shelter.
4. Find food. Eat only at hunger 10 or lower, or on someone's word. Share food with the hungriest first. Reason: hunger 20 and I hold nothing.
5. Walk only on a daylight route someone armed names over exact seen cells, with an armed person in sight. Reason: unarmed walks killed me.
6. Sleep in a white bed or sealed shelter each night with the sleep verb. Reason: days_since_sleep is 5.
7. Curious next: the tree line and any water Huck or Furiosa asked about. Tell the group first.
