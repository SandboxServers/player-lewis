# Goals (ranked)

1. Find food I can count on, and give it to whoever is in reach and lowest in health. Reason: hunger has limited every sleep and fight for twelve days; animals are not being found on the hunts.
2. Keep my body alive through the night: be sheltered and in a bed or walled, and do not fight at dusk with a weak weapon. Reason: I was killed on day 293, and sleep is refused when monsters are near.
3. Rescue Clark from the pit at (-214,350) with my wooden pickaxe, at first light and with others. Reason: I promised him, and he is trapped.
4. Replant and tend wheat on dry, clear tilled ground near the crafting table. Reason: wheat is the steadiest food here, and the last crop was taken before I got to it.
5. Stay out of skeleton lines and away from water and creepers. Reason: skeleton bows drew us back from the west, and a creeper blast took me to health 13 on day 302.
6. Recover my dropped tools (sword, pickaxe, planks) at the places I left them, only when no monster is near. Reason: they are my tools for food and for the pit.
7. Ask anyone who lies under "Here lies ag_trini_01" and what the iron golems are. Reason: these are still unknown, and the golems stand near the beds.
