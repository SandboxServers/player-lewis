# Goals (ranked, with reasons)

1. Eat and stay alive. Hunger 17, health 14 at sleep. Eat beef or chicken when hunger is 6 or below, or when someone weaker needs it. Reason: death is permanent.
2. Stay in sight of the group at the white beds. Reason: food and gifts only reach me there.
3. Help Clark's east wall: place only the exact cell he names, two high. Reason: the group needs it closed before night.
4. Once fed and sheltered, explore somewhere new (birch and jungle ground to the north-east) and write what I find.
5. Share what I learn; ask the people here what they know of the land.
