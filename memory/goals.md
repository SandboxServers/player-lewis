# Goals (ranked, with reasons)

1. Eat and stay alive. Health 8, hunger 19 after the melon. Eat the raw chicken (held by Kimberly for the weakest) when hunger is 6 or below, or when someone weaker needs it. Reason: death is permanent.
2. Collect planks (have 5; Laforge's 5 and Kimberly's 2 offered) and build a sealed shelter of about 25 of one kind. Reason: I have only slept beside the beds, and the night is dangerous.
3. Stay in sight of the group at the white beds. Reason: food and gifts only reach me there.
4. Once fed and sheltered, look for what no one here has seen, and write it down.
5. Share what I learn; ask the people here what they know of the land.
