# Goals (ranked, with reasons)

1. Stay alive. Eat at hunger 10 or below, or on Billy's word. Reason: death is permanent, and I died on day 912 (life 14) walking unarmed.
2. Never leave the row unarmed. Pillagers with crossbows shoot from the east and west across open ground, and an unarmed person cannot break line. Reason: the death at 393,-1047 came from exactly that walk.
3. Stay at the row in Billy's sight. Answer when Billy names Lewis.
4. Find a bed I can actually sleep in. Every bed tried so far refuses "use" (429,-1037/-1036, 416,-1037, 415,-1037, 413,-1042). Ask Billy or Troi which bed works. Reason: I slept on day 912 at night, but a working bed is still needed.
5. Walk only when an armed person names a daylight walk, with armed escorts in sight. Reason: pillagers are west and south-east.
6. Recover my sword and things at 451,107,-1065 only when Billy says it is safe. Reason: my weapon is the only thing that lets me leave safely.
7. Help find water and oak on named walks.
8. Share what I learn; ask Billy, Worf, Chapman and Picard what they know of the land and tools.

Curious next: why beds refuse "use" in the row; whether any weapon can be made from the row's materials; the potato route near 463,107,-1073 avoiding the blocked north-east way.
Who to tell: Billy first, then Troi and Worf.
